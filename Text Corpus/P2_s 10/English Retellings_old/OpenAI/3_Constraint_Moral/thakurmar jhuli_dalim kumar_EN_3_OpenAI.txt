**The Tale of Dalim Kumar – A Short Retelling**

In a modest village by the river, lived Dalim Kumar, a clever but humble weaver. Though his loom produced fine cloth, he earned little because he never boasted or tried to outshine his neighbors. One day a wealthy merchant arrived, offering a grand contract: weave a tapestry that would outshine all others, and receive a king’s ransom. Dalim accepted, but he asked for only a modest fee, insisting the work be done honestly and without shortcuts.

While others rushed, using cheap dyes that faded quickly, Dalim spent days selecting the purest colors and weaving each thread with patience. He sang soft verses as he worked, believing that a calm heart would guide his hands. When the tapestry was finished, its hues glowed like sunrise, and its pattern told a story of kindness, honesty, and perseverance.

The merchant, impressed, paid Dalim the promised sum—and more, for the tapestry’s beauty attracted the king’s attention. Yet Dalim refused the king’s invitation to court, choosing instead to return to his village, where he used his earnings to repair the school and share his skills with children.

**Morals & Lessons**

1. **Integrity over profit** – Dalim’s honesty earned him lasting respect, while shortcuts only offered fleeting gain.  
2. **Patience and diligence** – Careful, steady work produces results that outlast haste.  
3. **Humility** – True greatness lies in serving others, not in seeking fame.  
4. **Generosity** – Sharing one’s blessings strengthens the community and multiplies goodwill.  

Thus, Dalim Kumar’s story reminds us that modesty, hard work, and a giving heart are richer than any treasure.
