**Khumpui – The Clever Little Girl**

In a remote Bengali village lived a poor farmer and his wife, who had a tiny daughter named Khumpui. Though small, Khumpui possessed a sharp mind and a kind heart. One day, a greedy landlord demanded a hefty tax from the farmer, threatening to seize his fields if he could not pay. The farmer, helpless, confessed his plight to Khumpui.

Khumpui devised a plan. She went to the landlord’s house, pretending to be a wandering storyteller. She offered to tell a tale that would reveal the landlord’s hidden treasure—if he promised to forgive the farmer’s tax. Intrigued, the landlord agreed.

She began: “There is a magical banyan tree that bears golden fruits, but only those who share their wealth with the needy can pluck them.” The landlord, eager for gold, asked how to find it. Khumpui whispered, “The tree grows where generosity is sown.” She then suggested the landlord plant a seed of kindness by forgiving the farmer’s debt. The landlord, swayed by the story’s promise, relented.

The farmer’s fields were saved, and the landlord, humbled by Khumpui’s wisdom, learned that true wealth lies in compassion, not greed. Word of Khumpui’s cleverness spread, and she grew up to be a respected advisor, always reminding others that:

- **Cleverness can turn power into justice.**  
- **Generosity protects the community.**  
- **Even the smallest voice can change a great heart.**  

Thus, Khumpui’s tale endures as a lesson that wit, kindness, and humility are the richest treasures of all.
