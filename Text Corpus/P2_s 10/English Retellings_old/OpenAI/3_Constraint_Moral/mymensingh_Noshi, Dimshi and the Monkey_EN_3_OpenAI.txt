**Noshi, Dimshi and the Monkey – A Short Retelling**

In a quiet village, two sisters, Noshi and Dimshi, lived together. One day a mischievous monkey entered their courtyard, eyeing the ripe mangoes hanging from the tree. The monkey leapt, snatched a mango, and fled, laughing at the sisters’ helplessness.

Noshi, quick‑witted, whispered a plan to Dimshi. They gathered a sturdy rope, a bamboo pole, and a large leaf. Pretending to be frightened, they called out, “Oh, dear monkey, you’ve taken our mango! Return it, or we’ll call the village chief!” The curious monkey, proud of its daring, returned to hear what would happen.

Noshi tied the rope to the pole, looped the other end around the monkey’s tail, and covered it with the leaf. When the monkey tried to run, the rope tightened, and the leaf slipped, exposing the monkey’s tail. Embarrassed, the monkey dropped the mango and fled, never to return.

**Moral Lessons**

1. **Cleverness Trumps Strength** – Noshi’s ingenuity solved the problem without force, showing that brains can outwit brawn.
2. **Teamwork Amplifies Success** – The sisters’ cooperation turned a single idea into an effective plan.
3. **Pride Leads to Downfall** – The monkey’s boastful confidence made it vulnerable to a simple trap.
4. **Calm Resolve Over Panic** – By staying composed, Noshi and Dimshi avoided rash actions that could have worsened the situation.

Thus, the tale reminds us that thoughtful strategy, unity, and humility are more powerful than sheer might.
