Here's the Bengali folktale "Noshi, Dimshi and the Monkey" retold, focusing on its morals:

A greedy monkey discovered a sparkling diamond nose-ring. Thrilled by his find, he bragged to Noshi and Dimshi (a small bird and her chick). "Behold my priceless treasure!" he screeched.

Noshi and Dimshi advised caution: "Hide it safely, Monkey! Someone might steal it." 

But the arrogant monkey scoffed. "Bah! What do *you* know? I'll wear it proudly *right here*!" He stuck the ring onto a branch tip to admire it. Instantly, a strong wind blew it into the river, lost forever.

The monkey wailed in despair, realizing his foolishness too late.

**Morals/Lessons:**
1.  **Beware of Greed and Arrogance:** The monkey's greed blinded him, and his arrogance made him dismiss wise advice, leading directly to his loss.
2.  **Wisdom Knows No Size:** Humble creatures like Noshi and Dimshi often possess practical wisdom overlooked by the proud.
3.  **Heed Good Advice:** Ignoring sensible warnings, especially from those you consider lesser, can have costly consequences.
4.  **Act Prudently:** Showing off valuables carelessly invites trouble; securing them wisely is essential.
