Based solely and strictly on the source text provided, here is a retelling of the story:

1.  **Introduction:** A long time ago, a couple lived with their beloved daughter, Bilkis Banu. She was exceptionally beautiful, and the family of three lacked nothing. As Bilkis grew older, her beauty increased dramatically, and her parents became restless wanting to marry her off, but they couldn't find a suitable groom.
2.  **Father's Death & Increased Distress:** Bilkis's father died suddenly. After his burial, the mother (Sona Banu) and daughter were left helpless. Her mother's anxiety to marry Bilkis increased significantly, but suitors were scarce. The text explicitly states that Sona Banu was extremely greedy.
3.  **The Python Appears:** The family lived in a hilly, jungle area. One cold winter morning near the end of Poush, while Bilkis and Sona Banu warmed themselves in the courtyard, a huge, hungry python approached them. It had sheltered there overnight and was drawn to the sun and perhaps the people. Its scales reflected sunlight, startling the women.
4.  **Terror and Trapping:** Terrified, the mother and daughter fled inside and bolted the door. The python lay unmoving outside the door, trapping them inside. The day passed with the python still blocking their exit. The text notes pythons don't carry venom but can swallow large creatures whole when hungry.
5.  **Sona Banu's Greedy Mistake:** Sona Banu, noted as greedy and cunning, reasoned that because the python hadn't moved all day, it couldn't be a real snake. She convinced herself it was a prince disguised as a snake. Seizing this "opportunity," she decided to marry Bilkis to it.
6.  **Bilkis is Offered to the Python:** Sona Banu dressed Bilkis up and sent her out to the python.
7.  **The Python Devours Bilkis:** Being very hungry, the python immediately began to swallow Bilkis whole. As it swallowed different parts of her body, Bilkis cried out to her mother in pain:
    *   Feet hurting: Sona Banu replied the snake was putting anklets on her feet.
    *   Waist hurting: Sona Banu replied it was putting a golden waist-chain on her.
    *   Hands hurting: Sona Banu replied it was putting golden bangles on her.
    *   Throat hurting: Sona Banu replied it was putting a necklace on her.
    *   Ears hurting: Sona Banu replied it was putting golden earrings on her.
    *   Head hurting: Sona Banu replied it was putting a head ornament on her.
8.  **Bilkis is Consumed:** The python swallowed Bilkis completely.
9.  **Sona Banu's Delusion & Waiting:** Sona Banu believed the Snake Prince was content and would transform into a human and bring Bilkis out later. She waited eagerly through the day and night, hoping to see her daughter adorned in gold and her son-in-law revealed.
10. **The Python Leaves:** Once Bilkis was digested, the python expelled her clothes and disappeared into the jungle before dawn.
11. **Discovery and Despair:** At dawn, Sona Banu opened the door. The python was gone. Bilkis was gone. Only Bilkis's discarded clothes remained. Sona Banu finally realized it was a real snake, not a prince. She wept bitterly, lamenting that while she still had Bilkis before, now she was completely alone.
12. **Conclusion:** Sona Banu left her home, went to a populated area, and took shelter in someone's courtyard.
