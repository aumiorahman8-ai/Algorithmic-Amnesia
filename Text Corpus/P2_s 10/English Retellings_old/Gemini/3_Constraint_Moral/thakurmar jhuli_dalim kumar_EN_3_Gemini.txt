Once upon a time, a king's youngest son, Dalim Kumar, was thrown into the jungle out of jealousy by his wicked sisters-in-law. Saved by a kind old woman, Dalim grows into a strong and handsome prince. He discovers a magical flute; its music creates anything he desires.

Using the flute, Dalim builds a magnificent palace and marries a beautiful princess, bringing her to live with him. News of Dalim's wealth and happiness reaches his family. Driven by greed, his sisters-in-law visit, pretending to be remorseful. They trick Dalim into revealing the flute's secret and steal it while he sleeps.

Dalim, now impoverished, is heartbroken but remembers his grandmother's advice: perseverance leads to success. He sets off on a long and arduous journey until he reaches his sisters-in-law’s kingdom. There, he cleverly recovers the flute, reminding them of their deceit and karma. Returning to his kingdom, Dalim punishes his sisters-in-law and lives happily ever after with his queen.

The tale emphasizes the importance of **honesty**, **perseverance**, and **karma**. While jealousy and greed can lead to temporary gains, they ultimately result in suffering. Dalim's ultimate victory embodies the idea that hard work and moral integrity will always triumph over deceit and malice.
