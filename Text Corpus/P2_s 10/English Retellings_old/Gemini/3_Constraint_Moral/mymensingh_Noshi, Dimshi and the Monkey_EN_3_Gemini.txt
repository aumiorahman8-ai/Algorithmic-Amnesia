Noshi and Dimshi, two lazy sisters, were tasked with bringing vegetables to market. En route, they encountered a playful monkey who offered to transport the basket in exchange for a share of the profits. Greedy, the sisters agreed, believing the monkey would scare away buyers, leaving all proceeds to them.

The monkey, however, proved a shrewd trader. His entertaining antics drew crowds, and the vegetables sold out for a high price. Noshi and Dimshi, expecting to cheat the monkey, were stunned when he demanded his fair share. They tried to trick him, offering him the inferior vegetables.

Wise to their deception, the monkey scattered the remaining vegetables, showing how little he valued their dishonesty. He then disappeared into the forest, leaving the sisters empty-handed and filled with shame.

The tale teaches that greed and laziness lead to ruin. Honesty and hard work are more rewarding than trying to deceive others. Furthermore, it warns against underestimating those who appear different or less intelligent, as they may possess a wisdom that surpasses our own.
