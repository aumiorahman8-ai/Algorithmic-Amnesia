Nasi and Dimsi were walking down a deserted road when they heard a voice offering marriage. They ignored it at first, but the voice persisted. Eventually, they discovered it was a monkey jumping in the trees and tempting them with gifts if they married him.

They reached a wide canal they couldn't cross. Desperate, they promised to marry the monkey if he helped them. He agreed, carried them across one by one, but after reaching the other side they broke their promise.

The monkey followed, undeterred. The sisters then saw a woodpecker nest and told the monkey if he got them the baby birds, they would marry him. He agreed and climbed the tree but trapped his hand in the nest. He was dividing the chicks up for each sister. Impatient, the sisters grew annoyed and threatened to leave. In a panic, trying to get free, the monkey pulled so hard that his arm ripped off and he fell into thick mud, trapping him.
