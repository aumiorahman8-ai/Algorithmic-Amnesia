At the joining of the Mathabhanga river, it was typical for passing cargo ships to give a portion of their goods to the local goddess. One time, a merchant refused to give anything and insulted the goddess. As a result, his boat sank into the river.

People tried to find the bottom of the river with long ropes, but they could not reach it. A brave man then volunteered to dive. Underwater, he saw a golden boat and two fairies who were pulling on the rope used to find him. He pleaded for his boat back, and the fairies agreed, saying he’d find it at the Kazis' ghat, but warned him not to tell anyone what he had seen or he would die.

The boat was found where the fairies said it would be. However, the man couldn't keep silent and told his wife what had occurred. He then vomited blood and died, as the fairies had foretold.
