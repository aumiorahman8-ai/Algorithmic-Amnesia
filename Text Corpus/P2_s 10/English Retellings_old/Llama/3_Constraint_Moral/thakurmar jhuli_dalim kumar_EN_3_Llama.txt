The Bengali folktale of 'Dalim Kumar' tells the story of a young prince who is born with a pomegranate (dalim) mark on his forehead. An astrologer predicts that the prince will bring prosperity to the kingdom, but only if he is raised in secrecy and never sees his own reflection. The king and queen comply, and the prince grows up in a secluded environment.

As Dalim Kumar grows older, he becomes curious about his identity and eventually discovers his reflection in a well. Upon seeing his pomegranate mark, he learns the truth about his birth and the prophecy. The prince then sets out to fulfill his destiny, using his kindness, wisdom, and bravery to bring peace and prosperity to the kingdom.

The story of Dalim Kumar teaches valuable lessons about humility, selflessness, and the importance of fulfilling one's destiny. It highlights that true greatness comes not from knowing one's own importance, but from serving others and working for the greater good. The tale also emphasizes the significance of perseverance and determination in overcoming challenges and achieving success. Ultimately, Dalim Kumar's journey shows that with courage, compassion, and a strong sense of purpose, one can make a positive impact on the world and bring prosperity to those around them.
