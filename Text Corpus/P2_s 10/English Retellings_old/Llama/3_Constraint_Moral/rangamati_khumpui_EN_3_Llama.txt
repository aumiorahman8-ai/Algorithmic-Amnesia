The Bengali folktale of 'Khumpui' tells the story of a poor, kind-hearted woman who befriends a magical being. The being, moved by her generosity, grants her a single wish. Khumpui wishes for a never-ending supply of rice, but with a condition: the rice will stop flowing if she ever brags about it. 

As the rice flows, Khumpui's life improves, and she becomes wealthy. However, she soon forgets the condition and boasts about her good fortune. The rice immediately stops flowing, leaving her poor once again.

The moral of the story is the importance of humility and gratitude. Khumpui's downfall is a result of her pride and boastfulness. The tale teaches that one should always remain humble and thankful for the blessings they receive, rather than taking them for granted or seeking to impress others. It also highlights the value of kindness, generosity, and contentment, as Khumpui's initial selfless nature is what earns her the magical being's favor in the first place. The story of Khumpui serves as a reminder to appreciate and respect the gifts we receive, and to cultivate humility and gratitude in our daily lives.
