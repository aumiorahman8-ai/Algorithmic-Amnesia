In the Bengali folktale "Marriage with the Prince of Snake," a poor farmer's daughter is forced to marry a snake prince due to a debt owed by her father. Despite initial reluctance, the daughter treats the snake with kindness and respect, and he transforms into a handsome prince. However, when she fails to keep her promise to him, he returns to his snake form.

The tale teaches valuable lessons about the importance of honesty, loyalty, and keeping one's word. The daughter's kindness and compassion towards the snake, despite his appearance, demonstrate the value of empathy and understanding. The story also highlights the consequences of breaking promises and the importance of integrity.

Moreover, the tale suggests that true beauty and worth lie within, and that appearances can be deceiving. The snake prince's transformation into a handsome prince symbolizes the potential for personal growth and transformation that can occur when treated with kindness and respect. Ultimately, the story promotes a message of selflessness, forgiveness, and the rewards of treating others with dignity and compassion, regardless of their appearance or background.
