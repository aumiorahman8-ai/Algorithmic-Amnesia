In the Bengali folktale 'Noshi, Dimshi and the Monkey,' two friends, Noshi and Dimshi, befriend a mischievous monkey. The monkey tricks them into giving him their food and belongings, and eventually, they lose everything. The story teaches valuable lessons about the consequences of greed, gullibility, and the importance of being cautious when dealing with strangers.

The tale highlights the dangers of being too trusting and naive, as Noshi and Dimshi's kindness is taken advantage of by the cunning monkey. It also emphasizes the importance of being mindful of one's actions and their potential consequences. The story serves as a warning to be careful when interacting with others, especially those with ulterior motives.

Moreover, the folktale promotes the value of self-reliance and resourcefulness. After losing everything, Noshi and Dimshi must work together to regain their losses and learn from their mistakes. The story encourages children to think critically and develop problem-solving skills to overcome challenges. Ultimately, 'Noshi, Dimshi and the Monkey' teaches children essential life lessons about responsibility, caution, and the importance of learning from one's mistakes.
