A man murdered his enemy and buried the body in a pit near his house. Years passed, and the case was forgotten since no one found any clues about the killer. One day, during the monsoon, the man was sitting on his veranda and noticed bubbles rising from the spot where he had buried the body. He started smiling to himself, thinking that he had gotten away with the crime. His wife noticed his smile and pressed him to reveal the reason. Eventually, he confessed to her that he had killed and buried someone in that spot, and he was laughing because he thought he had escaped punishment.

However, the man's confession ultimately led to his downfall. He had a quarrel with his wife, and in a fit of rage, she revealed his secret to the entire village by screaming that he was a murderer. The news spread quickly, and the relatives of the murdered man reported the crime to the police. The man was arrested, and in the end, he received the punishment he deserved for his crime.
