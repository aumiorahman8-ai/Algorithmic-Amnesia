At the confluence of the Mathabhanga river, merchants would traditionally offer a portion of their goods to the goddess as they passed by. However, one merchant refused to do so and even insulted the goddess, causing his cargo boat to sink. Attempts to measure the river's depth and recover the boat were unsuccessful, with divers too afraid to descend. Eventually, a brave man volunteered to go down into the river, where he found a golden boat with two fairies pulling on the rope that had been lowered to measure the depth.

The fairies told the man that he would get his boat back if he kept the encounter a secret, warning him that if he told anyone, he would vomit blood and die. They instructed him to find the boat at the Kazis' ghat. The boat was indeed found there, but the man couldn't resist telling his wife about his experience. Unfortunately, this led to his downfall, as he vomited blood and died, fulfilling the fairies' prophecy.
