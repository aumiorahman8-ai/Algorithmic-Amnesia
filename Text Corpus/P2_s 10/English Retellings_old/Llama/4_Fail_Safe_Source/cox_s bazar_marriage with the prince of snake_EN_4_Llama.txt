Here's the retelling of the story based on the source text:

A long time ago, a couple lived with their beautiful daughter, Bilkis Banu, in a hilly area surrounded by a dense jungle. After the father's death, Bilkis's mother, Sona Banu, became anxious to get her married, but no suitor came forward. One winter morning, a huge python appeared in their courtyard, and the mother and daughter took shelter inside the house, bolting the door. The python lay motionless at the door, and Sona Banu, being greedy and cunning, thought it might be a prince in disguise.

Believing this, Sona Banu decided to marry her daughter to the "Snake Prince." She dressed up Bilkis and sent her to the python, which began to swallow her. As the snake swallowed Bilkis's feet, waist, hands, neck, ears, and head, she cried out in pain, but her mother reassured her that the Snake Prince was putting on golden ornaments. Eventually, the python swallowed Bilkis whole, and Sona Banu waited for the Snake Prince to reveal himself and bring out her daughter, adorned with gold jewelry.

However, the python simply lay motionless, and as night fell, Sona Banu fell asleep. The python digested Bilkis, regurgitated her clothes, and disappeared into the jungle before dawn. When Sona Banu woke up, she found the python and her daughter gone, with only Bilkis's clothes remaining. Realizing her mistake, Sona Banu lamented and wept, finally understanding that the python was not a prince but a real snake. Overcome with grief and loneliness, Sona Banu left her home and took shelter in a populated area, abandoning her house.
