Two sisters, Nasi and Dimsi, were walking down a deserted road when they heard a voice calling out to them, asking them to marry. They ignored it at first, but eventually saw a monkey jumping from tree to tree, following them and making proposals. The monkey would offer them gifts, including a water pot, in exchange for marriage. The sisters paid him little attention until they came to a wide canal that they couldn't cross. In exchange for the monkey's help in crossing the canal, they promised to marry him, but after he successfully carried them across, they reneged on their promise, saying they only said it to get across.

The monkey didn't give up and continued to follow them. When the sisters expressed a desire to raise some baby birds from a woodpecker's nest, they again promised to marry the monkey if he could get them the birds. The monkey climbed the tree, but got his hand stuck in the nest while trying to count the chicks and divide them between the sisters. As the sisters waited and grew impatient, the monkey became desperate and tried to pull his hand out, but it was stuck. In a final attempt, he used all his might and managed to tear his arm off, leaving it in the nest, and fell into the mud below, where he became stuck.
