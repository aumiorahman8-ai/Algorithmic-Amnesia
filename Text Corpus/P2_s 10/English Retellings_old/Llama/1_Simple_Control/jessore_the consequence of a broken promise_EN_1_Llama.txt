The Bengali folktale 'The Consequence of a Broken Promise' is a story that has been passed down through generations, teaching the importance of keeping one's word. Here's a retelling of the tale:

Once upon a time, in a small village in Bengal, there lived a poor farmer named Kalo. Kalo was a hardworking man who tilled the land and grew crops to feed his family. One day, while working in his field, Kalo stumbled upon a hidden treasure buried beneath the earth. Overjoyed by his discovery, he decided to use the treasure to improve his life and the lives of his loved ones.

However, as Kalo was about to leave the field, he heard a voice behind him. It was the voice of the goddess of the land, Dharti Ma. She appeared before Kalo and said, "I have been watching you, Kalo. You are a good man, and I am pleased with your honesty. I will grant you a single wish, but you must promise to use the treasure wisely and never forget the poor and needy."

Kalo, overwhelmed with gratitude, promised to use the treasure to help those in need and to always remember the goddess's kindness. Dharti Ma, satisfied with Kalo's promise, vanished, leaving him to enjoy his newfound wealth.

At first, Kalo kept his promise. He used the treasure to build a new home, feed the hungry, and help the poor. However, as time passed, Kalo began to forget the goddess's words. He became greedy and started to use the treasure only for his own benefit. He built a grand house, bought expensive clothes, and threw lavish parties, but he forgot about the poor and needy.

One day, a severe drought struck the land, and the crops began to wither away. The villagers, including Kalo's family, suffered greatly. Kalo, realizing his mistake, remembered the promise he had made to Dharti Ma. He tried to make amends by helping the poor and needy, but it was too late. The goddess, displeased with Kalo's broken promise, appeared before him once again.

Dharti Ma said, "Kalo, you have forgotten the promise you made to me. You have used the treasure for your own selfish desires, and now you must face the consequences. Your wealth will disappear, and you will be left with nothing."

And so, the treasure vanished, leaving Kalo with nothing but the clothes on his back. The villagers, who had once looked up to Kalo as a generous and kind man, now shunned him. Kalo was left to wander the streets, a poor and broken man, regretting the promise he had broken.

The story of Kalo serves as a reminder of the importance of keeping one's word and the consequences of breaking a promise. It teaches us that our actions have repercussions and that we must always be mindful of the impact our decisions have on others and on ourselves.
