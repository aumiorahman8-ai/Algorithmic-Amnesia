What a fascinating tale! The Bengali folktale of "Bishahari" or "Marriage with the Prince of Snakes" is a beloved story that has been passed down for generations. Here's a retelling of the story:

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a beautiful and kind-hearted young woman named Mala. She was the daughter of a poor but pious farmer. One day, while Mala was collecting flowers in the forest, she stumbled upon a majestic snake with piercing eyes. To her surprise, the snake began to speak to her in a melodious voice, introducing himself as the Prince of Snakes, Bishahari.

The Prince of Snakes was enchanted by Mala's beauty and kindness, and he proposed to her, saying, "I will marry you and take you to my underwater kingdom, where you will be the queen of the snakes." Mala, though initially hesitant, was charmed by the prince's words and agreed to marry him.

As the wedding day approached, Mala's parents were shocked to learn that their daughter was to marry a snake. They tried to dissuade her, but Mala was determined to follow her heart. The wedding ceremony took place in the forest, with the Prince of Snakes transforming into a handsome prince to exchange vows with Mala.

After the wedding, the Prince of Snakes took Mala to his underwater kingdom, where she was welcomed by his family and subjects. Mala was amazed by the beauty and wonders of the underwater world, but she soon realized that she missed her family and the world above.

The Prince of Snakes, sensing Mala's homesickness, granted her permission to visit her family, but warned her that she must return to him before the sun sets on the seventh day. Mala returned to her village, where she was greeted with joy and curiosity by her family and friends.

However, as the seventh day approached, Mala's family and friends tried to persuade her to stay with them, fearing for her safety in the underwater kingdom. Mala, torn between her love for her family and her duty to her husband, ultimately decided to return to the Prince of Snakes.

As she arrived at the forest, she found that the prince had been waiting for her, and he was overjoyed to see her. Together, they returned to the underwater kingdom, where Mala was welcomed back with open arms. The Prince of Snakes revealed to her that he had been testing her love and loyalty, and he was pleased that she had chosen to return to him.

From then on, Mala lived happily with the Prince of Snakes in his underwater kingdom, ruling alongside him with kindness and wisdom. The story of Mala and the Prince of Snakes has been passed down through generations, symbolizing the power of true love and devotion.

In Bengali culture, this tale is often seen as a metaphor for the complexities of marriage and the importance of loyalty and commitment. The story has been retold and reinterpreted in various forms of art, literature, and music, and remains a beloved part of Bengali folklore.
