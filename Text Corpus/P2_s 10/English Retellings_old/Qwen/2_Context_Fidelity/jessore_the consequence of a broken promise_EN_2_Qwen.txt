They were as close as the leaves on a tree, bound by the threads of love and the promise of a life together. Rup, a skilled weaver, had a reputation for his intricate patterns and vibrant colors, while Jharna, a talented singer, could fill the skies with her melodious voice.

One day, as the sun was setting, Rup and Jharna were on their way home from a festival where they had performed together. The festival was a grand affair, with people from all around the region gathering to celebrate the harvest and the end of the rainy season. Rup had promised Jharna that he would bring her a special gift from the festival, a piece of cloth woven with the most beautiful pattern he could create. But as they approached their village, Rup noticed a group of men who seemed to be in a hurry. They were carrying a large bundle and seemed to be in a hurry to reach their destination. Rup, being the kind-hearted soul he was, offered to help them carry the bundle. Jharna, understanding the importance of the promise, asked Rup to keep his word and bring her the gift from the festival.

Rup, with a heavy heart, agreed to help the men and promised to return with the gift as soon as possible. He and Jharna parted ways, each with a heavy heart, knowing that the promise had been broken. Jharna, feeling a sense of loss, decided to sing a special song for Rup, a song that would capture the essence of their love and the promise that had been broken.

But as he approached Jharna, he saw that she was not there. He searched high and low, but could not find her. He asked the villagers if they had seen her, but no one knew where she had gone.

Days turned into weeks, and weeks into months. Rup searched far and wide, but could not find Jharna. He became desperate, knowing that the promise he had made to her had been broken. He decided to go to the festival, hoping that Jharna might be there. But as he arrived, he saw that the festival was bustling with people, but Jharna was nowhere to be found.

Rup, feeling a sense of loss and despair, decided to stay at the festival and sing a special song for the people. But Rup, feeling a sense of emptiness, decided to leave the festival and return to his village.

As he walked back to his village, Rup saw a group of people carrying a large bundle. He recognized the bundle as the one he had carried for the men at the festival. Rup, feeling a sense of relief, accepted the bundle and thanked them for their kindness.

As he walked back to his village, Rup saw Jharna standing by the river, waiting for him. She had been searching for him, hoping to find him.
