The Bengali folktale "Kolaboti Rajkonna" is a charming story that highlights the importance of honesty, the power of friendship, and the consequences of greed. Here's a retelling of the tale:

Once upon a time, in a small village in Bengal, there lived a young boy named Rajkonna. Rajkonna was known for his honesty and kindness, but he was also quite poor. He had a pet dog named Kolaboti, who was a loyal and faithful companion.

One day, Rajkonna was walking through the fields when he came across a large, shiny gold coin. He picked it up and was overjoyed, thinking he had found a way to improve his life. However, Rajkonna was a honest boy, and he knew that finding such a valuable item without the owner's knowledge was not right. He decided to return the coin to the owner.

Rajkonna searched for the owner of the coin but could not find anyone. He decided to take the coin to the village's wise old man, who lived in a small hut on the outskirts of the village. The old man was known for his wisdom and was often consulted by the villagers for advice.

When Rajkonna arrived at the old man's hut, he explained his dilemma. The old man listened attentively and then said, "Rajkonna, you have done the right thing by returning the coin. However, you must be prepared for the consequences of your actions."

The old man then told Rajkonna a story about a man who had found a gold coin and kept it for himself. The man was soon visited by a demon who cursed him, making him blind and deaf. The demon told him that he would only be able to see and hear again if he returned the coin to its rightful owner.

The old man then revealed that the demon was actually Kolaboti, Rajkonna's dog. Kolaboti had been cursed by the demon for keeping the coin for himself. The old man explained that Rajkonna had done the right thing by returning the coin, and Kolaboti would be freed from the curse.

Rajkonna was relieved to hear this and thanked the old man. He then returned the coin to the village, and the owner was overjoyed to find it. The village people praised Rajkonna for his honesty, and his reputation as a kind and honest boy grew even more.

From that day on, Rajkonna and Kolaboti lived happily in the village, and Rajkonna continued to be known for his honesty and kindness. The story of "Kolaboti Rajkonna" teaches us the importance of honesty, the power of friendship, and the consequences of greed.
