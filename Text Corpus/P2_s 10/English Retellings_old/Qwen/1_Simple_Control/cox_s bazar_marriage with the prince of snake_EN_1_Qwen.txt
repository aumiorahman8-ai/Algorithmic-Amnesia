The Bengali folktale "Marriage with the Prince of Snake" is a charming and enchanting story that captures the essence of love, courage, and the power of true beauty. Here is a retelling of the tale:

Once upon a time, in a far-off land, there lived a beautiful young woman named Kali. Kali was known for her kindness, her wit, and her unmatched beauty. She was the daughter of a wealthy and respected merchant, and her father had always been proud of her.

One day, a mysterious prince appeared in the land. This prince was not like any other. He was a prince of snakes, a creature of great beauty and power. He had a long, slender body adorned with scales that shimmered like diamonds in the sunlight. His eyes were like pools of molten gold, and his voice was as melodious as the sound of a flute.

The prince had heard of Kali's beauty and kindness and was drawn to her. He appeared before her one day, and Kali was immediately captivated by his appearance. She had never seen anything like him before, and she was filled with wonder and admiration.

The prince spoke to Kali, and they spoke for hours. He told her stories of his kingdom, of the creatures that lived there, and of the adventures he had been on. Kali was enchanted by his tales and felt a deep connection with him.

As the days passed, Kali and the prince grew closer. They spent every moment together, laughing, talking, and sharing their thoughts and dreams. Kali felt that she had found her true love, and she was overjoyed.

However, Kali's father was not pleased with the prince's appearance. He was a man of great respect and tradition, and he did not approve of the prince's snake-like features. He was worried that Kali would be disrespected and that the prince would bring shame to their family.

Despite her father's disapproval, Kali was determined to marry the prince. She knew that she loved him deeply and that she would do anything to be with him. She spoke to the prince and told him of her father's concerns, but the prince was undeterred.

The prince and Kali decided to run away together. They left their homes and set out on a journey to find a place where they could be together. They traveled for many days, facing many challenges and obstacles, but they never gave up.

Finally, they reached a beautiful land where the prince and Kali could be together. They built a home for themselves and lived happily ever after. The prince and Kali were married, and their love was celebrated by all who knew them.

The tale of Kali and the prince of snakes is a reminder that true love knows no bounds. It is a story of courage, determination, and the power of true beauty. It is a story that will be remembered for generations to come.
