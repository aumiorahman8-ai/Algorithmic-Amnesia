The Bengali folktale "Fifteen Sizzles, Three to Eat, The She-goat's Sign Is By The Binna Tree" is a charming story that teaches a valuable lesson about honesty, responsibility, and the importance of keeping one's word. Here's a retelling of the tale:

Once upon a time, in a village nestled among the rolling hills of Bengal, there lived a kind-hearted shepherd named Bhabu. Bhabu was known for his gentle nature and his love for animals, especially goats. One day, while tending to his flock, he noticed a she-goat that seemed to be in distress. The she-goat was weak and had lost her way. Bhabu took pity on her and decided to bring her back to his home.

As Bhabu was tending to the she-goat, he noticed a peculiar sign by a tree called the Binna Tree. The sign read, "Fifteen Sizzles, Three to Eat, The She-goat's Sign Is By The Binna Tree." Bhabu was puzzled by the sign but decided to follow it anyway, as he believed in the power of signs and omens.

Following the sign, Bhabu led the she-goat to a nearby village where he found a wise old man. The old man explained that the sign was a message from the gods, warning Bhabu that he would soon be faced with a difficult choice. The sign meant that Bhabu would have to make a decision that would affect the lives of his flock and the village.

Bhabu was confused and asked the old man to explain further. The old man revealed that the sign was a test of Bhabu's honesty and responsibility. He told Bhabu that if he were to eat three of the fifteen sizzles mentioned in the sign, it would mean that he was not being truthful and responsible with his flock. However, if he were to eat only three sizzles and leave the rest, it would mean that he was being honest and responsible.

Bhabu, being a kind and honest man, decided to eat only three sizzles and leave the rest. He knew that he had to be responsible for the well-being of his flock and the village. The old man praised Bhabu for his honesty and responsibility and blessed him with good fortune.

From that day on, Bhabu was known as a wise and responsible shepherd. He continued to tend to his flock with care and love, and the village prospered under his guidance. The story of the fifteen sizzles and the she-goat's sign by the Binna Tree became a well-known tale in the village, teaching future generations the importance of honesty, responsibility, and keeping one's word.
