The Bengali folktale "Noshi, Dimshi, and the Monkey" is a charming story that teaches the importance of honesty, friendship, and the value of one's word. Here's a retelling of the tale:

---

Once upon a time, in a village nestled between the hills and the river, there lived two friends, Noshi and Dimshi. They were inseparable, always playing together and helping each other. One day, they decided to go on a journey to the nearby forest to find a rare fruit that was said to be very sweet and healing.

As they ventured deeper into the forest, they came across a beautiful tree with fruits that looked like they were made of gold. The fruits were so shiny and tempting that Noshi and Dimshi couldn't resist. They picked a few and decided to taste them.

The fruits were indeed very sweet, and Noshi and Dimshi were delighted. However, as they were eating, they noticed a monkey watching them from a nearby tree. The monkey was very hungry and wanted the fruits too. Noshi and Dimshi, being kind-hearted, decided to share their fruits with the monkey.

The monkey, seeing their generosity, was very grateful. It promised to help them in return. Noshi and Dimshi were curious about what the monkey could do, but they trusted the monkey's word and agreed to the arrangement.

As they continued their journey, they encountered many challenges, including a deep river that they had to cross. The monkey, true to its word, appeared and helped them cross the river by carrying them on its back. Noshi and Dimshi were amazed and grateful for the monkey's help.

After crossing the river, they continued their journey and eventually reached the village. Noshi and Dimshi were very happy and proud of their adventure. They shared their story with everyone in the village, and everyone admired their bravery and the monkey's help.

However, as they were recounting their story, the monkey appeared again. It was very hungry and asked for some of the fruits they had left. Noshi and Dimshi, remembering the monkey's promise, were hesitant. But the monkey reminded them of its promise to help them in return.

Noshi and Dimshi, being true to their word, gave the monkey the fruits. The monkey was very happy and thanked them profusely. It then disappeared into the forest, promising to return and help them again in the future.

From that day on, Noshi and Dimshi and the monkey became very close friends. They continued to visit each other and help each other whenever needed. The story of Noshi, Dimshi, and the monkey spread far and wide, teaching everyone the importance of keeping one's word and the value of true friendship.

---

This folktale beautifully illustrates the virtues of honesty, the power of friendship, and the importance of keeping one's promises.
