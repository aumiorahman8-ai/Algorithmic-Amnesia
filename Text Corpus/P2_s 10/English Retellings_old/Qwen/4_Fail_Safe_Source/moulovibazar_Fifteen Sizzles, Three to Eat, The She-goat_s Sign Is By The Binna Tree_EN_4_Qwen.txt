A husband and wife were quarreling. The wife was making pithas, and every time she placed one on the griddle, it made a sizzling sound. The husband, listening, counted this sound fifteen times. The wife served her husband three pithas. Seeing the three pithas, the husband said to his wife, "Fifteen sizzles, three to eat."

The wife thought her husband was a fortune-teller and announced this to the neighborhood. A woman lost her goat and asked the man to find it. The husband told her to return in the afternoon. After searching, he found the goat near a binna tree. When the woman returned, the husband said, "Fifteen sizzles, three to eat, the she-goat's sign is by the binna tree." The woman found her goat and the man was known as Gonok Thakur, the Revered Fortune-teller.
