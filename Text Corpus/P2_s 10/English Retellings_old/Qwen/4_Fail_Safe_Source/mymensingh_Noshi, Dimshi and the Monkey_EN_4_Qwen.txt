Two sisters, Nasi and Dimsi, were walking down a deserted road when they heard someone calling out to them. Ignoring the voice, they continued walking. The call repeated, and they heard it again, this time clearly: "Hey Nasi, hey Dimsi, will you marry me?" The younger sister, Dimsi, noticed the voice and they both looked around, but saw no one. They walked a bit further and heard the same words again. They looked carefully and saw a monkey following them, jumping from tree to tree and playing tricks from behind cover. The monkey tried to tempt them with promises of gifts if they would marry him. 

As they walked, they came to a large canal that was too wide to jump across and too deep to wade through. The sisters were worried about how to get across. They offered the monkey a deal: if he could get them across the canal, one of them would marry him. The monkey agreed and swam across the canal, carrying each sister one at a time on his back. The sisters, however, were not interested in marrying the monkey and told him they only said they would marry him to get across the canal. The monkey was disappointed but continued to follow them.

The sisters then spotted a woodpecker's nest high up in a tree, and the chirping of baby birds filled the air. They were inspired by the sound and decided to marry the monkey if he could get them the baby birds. The monkey climbed the tree and stuck his hand inside the nest, counting the chicks. The sisters waited expectantly, but the monkey didn't come down. They asked him why he wasn't coming down, and he said he was counting the chicks. After a long time, the sisters grew impatient and told the monkey they didn't need the baby birds anymore and were leaving. The monkey, distressed by their decision, tried to pull his hand out of the nest but couldn't. He used all his strength, but his arm was torn from his body, and he fell into the thick mud below, unable to get out.
