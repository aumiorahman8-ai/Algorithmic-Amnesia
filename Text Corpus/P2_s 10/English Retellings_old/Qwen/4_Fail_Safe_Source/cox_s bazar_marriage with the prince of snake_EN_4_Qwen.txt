A long time ago, in a certain country, a family lived with their beloved daughter, Bilkis Banu. The family consisted of the mother, the father, and the daughter. Bilkis was incredibly beautiful, and the family was well-off. As she grew older, her beauty increased, and the family became anxious to marry her off. However, they could not find a suitable groom. After the father passed away, the mother, Sona Banu, became even more anxious to marry Bilkis. One winter morning, a huge python entered their courtyard, seeking warmth. The mother and daughter, unaware of the danger, were trapped inside the house. The python lay motionless at the door, and the mother, Sona Banu, thought it was a prince in disguise. She dressed Bilkis and sent her to the "prince." The python, hungry, swallowed Bilkis. The mother comforted Bilkis, saying the snake was putting jewelry on her. Eventually, the python swallowed Bilkis completely. The mother waited for the snake to release her daughter, but it did not. The python regurgitated Bilkis's clothes and disappeared. The mother realized the snake was real and wept for her daughter. She left her home and took shelter in a populated area.
