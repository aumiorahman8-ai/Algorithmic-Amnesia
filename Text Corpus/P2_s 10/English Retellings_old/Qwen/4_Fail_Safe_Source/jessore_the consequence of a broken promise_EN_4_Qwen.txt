A merchant vessel passing by the Mathabhanga river was required to offer a portion of its cargo to the goddess. One day, a merchant did not comply with this custom and made insulting remarks. As a result, his boat sank. Divers were brought in to recover the boat, but they could not measure the river's depth, even with a long rope. A brave man volunteered to dive into the river. He found a golden boat with two fairies pulling on a rope. The fairies told him that he would get his boat back if he promised not to tell anyone about the encounter. The man, however, could not resist telling his wife, which led to his death.
