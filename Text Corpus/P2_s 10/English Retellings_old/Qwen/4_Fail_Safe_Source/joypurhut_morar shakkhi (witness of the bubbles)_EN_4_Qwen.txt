A man murdered his enemy and buried the body in a spot near his house. Over the years, the incident was forgotten. One day, during a monsoon, the man was sitting on his veranda, and he noticed bubbles rising from the ground where he had buried the body. He began to laugh, and his wife noticed this. When she asked why he was laughing, he initially said it was for no reason. However, when she persisted, he revealed that he had killed his enemy and buried him in that spot. He laughed because no one had caught him as the killer, and he thought the saying "Sin never stays hidden" was false. Later, the man had a quarrel with his wife, and she screamed that he was a wicked man who had murdered his enemy. The news spread quickly, and the relatives of the murdered man reported the matter to the police. The police arrested the man, and he received his punishment.
