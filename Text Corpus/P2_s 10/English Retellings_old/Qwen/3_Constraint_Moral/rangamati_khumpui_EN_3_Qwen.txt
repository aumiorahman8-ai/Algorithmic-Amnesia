In the heart of a dense forest, there lived a wise old owl named Khumpui. Khumpui was known for his keen eyesight and sharp wit, which he used to solve the problems of the forest creatures. One day, a young deer, feeling lost and scared, approached Khumpui for guidance. The deer had wandered too far from its herd and was now surrounded by predators.

Khumpui listened to the deer's tale with great empathy. He then suggested a plan: the deer should run in a zigzag pattern, making it difficult for the predators to catch it. The deer, though hesitant at first, followed Khumpui's advice. As the predators chased, the zigzag pattern confused them, and the deer managed to escape.

The deer returned to its herd, grateful for Khumpui's wisdom. The story spread, and Khumpui became a symbol of guidance and wisdom in the forest. The moral of the tale is that sometimes, the best way to solve a problem is to think outside the box. It teaches us the importance of seeking advice and using our wits to overcome challenges. Khumpui's story also highlights the value of wisdom and the role of a wise elder in guiding younger generations.
