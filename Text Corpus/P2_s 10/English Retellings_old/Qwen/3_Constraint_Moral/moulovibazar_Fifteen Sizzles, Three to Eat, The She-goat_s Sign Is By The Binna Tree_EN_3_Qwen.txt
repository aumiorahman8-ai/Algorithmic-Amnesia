In the lush, verdant hills of Bengal, there once lived a she-goat named Binna. Binna was known for her gentle nature and her ability to find the most succulent grass. One day, while grazing, Binna noticed a peculiar sign by a tree. The sign read, "Fifteen Sizzles, Three to Eat, The She-goat's Sign Is By The Binna Tree." Intrigued, Binna decided to investigate.

As she approached the tree, she saw a group of fifteen sizzles (a type of wild boar) gathered around. They were indeed eating, but Binna noticed that three of them were eating more than the others. This raised a question in her mind: why were these three sizzles eating more than the rest?

Binna, being the wise and observant she-goat, realized that the sign was a warning. It was a message from the sizzles themselves, indicating that the three who were eating more were not part of the group and were likely to be a threat. She wisely decided to stay away from the group and continued her journey.

The moral of the story is clear: one should always be cautious and discerning, especially when dealing with strangers or unfamiliar situations. Just as Binna recognized the danger and avoided the sizzles, one should be wary of those who act differently from the group. This tale teaches us the importance of being observant and making wise decisions based on our observations.
