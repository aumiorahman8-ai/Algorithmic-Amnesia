In the village of Kali, there once lived a young boy named Rup, who was known for his honesty and integrity. One day, a wealthy merchant came to the village and offered Rup a job as his apprentice. The merchant was impressed by Rup's diligence and promised to give him a reward if he completed a specific task. However, the merchant broke his promise, leaving Rup with nothing.

Rup, feeling betrayed, decided to leave the village and seek his fortune elsewhere. He traveled far and wide, but his luck was not good. He met with many hardships and setbacks, and his journey was filled with trials. Eventually, Rup returned to his village, but he was no longer the same boy who had left. He had become bitter and resentful, and he vowed never to trust anyone again.

The villagers, who had always admired Rup's honesty, were shocked to see the change in him. They tried to console him, but Rup would not listen. He was consumed by his anger and frustration, and he refused to forgive the merchant who had broken his promise.

Years passed, and Rup's bitterness only grew stronger. He became a grumpy old man, and he spent his days complaining about the world and the people in it. One day, a young boy came to the village, and he was told of Rup's story. The young boy was moved by Rup's tale and decided to visit him.

When the young boy arrived, Rup was sitting alone, lost in thought. The young boy approached him and asked if he could sit with him. Rup, at first, was reluctant, but the young boy's kindness and sincerity eventually won him over. They spent the day talking, and Rup realized that he had been wrong to hold onto his anger for so long.

The young boy left, and Rup felt a sense of peace he hadn't felt in years. He realized that the consequences of a broken promise can be far-reaching, and that forgiveness is the only way to move forward. From that day on, Rup became a different person, and he spent the rest of his days spreading kindness and forgiveness to others.
