In the heart of a dense forest, there once lived three monkeys named Noshi, Dimshi, and their mother, Mashi. Mashi, wise and experienced, taught her children the importance of unity and cooperation. One day, while searching for food, they stumbled upon a large, ripe mango tree. The mangoes were so abundant that they could not be eaten all at once.

Noshi, the eldest, suggested they should divide the mangoes equally among themselves. Dimshi, the youngest, agreed, but Mashi, being the mother, proposed they should share the mangoes with the other animals in the forest. This idea was met with skepticism from Noshi and Dimshi, who thought they should keep the mangoes for themselves.

Mashi, however, insisted on sharing, explaining that the forest was their home and that all creatures should be treated with kindness and respect. She reminded them of the importance of helping others and the joy that comes from sharing.

Moved by her words, Noshi and Dimshi agreed to share the mangoes with the other animals. As they distributed the mangoes, they noticed that the other animals were grateful and happy. The forest became a place of harmony and joy, with all creatures living in peace.

The moral of the story is that sharing and kindness bring happiness and peace. It teaches us the value of cooperation and the importance of treating others with respect and kindness, even when it is not always convenient.
