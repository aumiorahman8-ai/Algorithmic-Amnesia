**Retelling of the Story:**

A long time ago, in a certain country, a couple lived happily with their only daughter, Bilkis Banu, who was exceptionally beautiful. The family had everything they needed, but as Bilkis grew older, her parents became anxious to find her a suitable husband. Unfortunately, before they could arrange her marriage, Bilkis’s father passed away, leaving her and her mother, Sona Banu, alone and helpless.

Sona Banu, who was greedy and cunning, became even more desperate to marry off her daughter. One cold winter morning, as they sat in their courtyard warming themselves in the sun, a massive python slithered toward them. Terrified, they ran inside and bolted the door, but the snake remained stretched out at the entrance.

Sona Banu, seeing the python’s motionless state, convinced herself that it was not a real snake but a prince in disguise. Eager to marry her daughter to this "Snake Prince," she dressed Bilkis in fine clothes and sent her out to him. The hungry python immediately began swallowing Bilkis, starting with her feet.

As the snake consumed her, Bilkis cried out in pain, describing each part of her body being swallowed. Each time, her mother reassured her with lies, claiming the snake was adorning her with jewelry—anklets for her feet, a golden waist-chain, bangles, a necklace, earrings, and a head ornament. Despite Bilkis’s pleas, her mother refused to intervene, believing the snake would later reveal itself as a prince and release her daughter.

After swallowing Bilkis completely, the python lay motionless. Sona Banu waited all day and night, hoping her daughter would emerge adorned in gold and her son-in-law would appear in human form. But by dawn, the snake had vanished, leaving only Bilkis’s clothes behind. Realizing her terrible mistake, Sona Banu wept in grief, now left completely alone. Overwhelmed with sorrow, she abandoned her home and wandered to a populated area, seeking shelter in a stranger’s courtyard.

Thus, the greedy mother’s foolishness led to the loss of her beloved daughter, leaving her with nothing but regret.
