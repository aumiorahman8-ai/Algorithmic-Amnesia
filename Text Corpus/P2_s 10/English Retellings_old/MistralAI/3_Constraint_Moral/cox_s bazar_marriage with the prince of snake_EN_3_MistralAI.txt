**The Marriage with the Snake Prince**

In a Bengali village, a poor but kind-hearted girl named Behula lived with her parents. One day, while fetching water, she accidentally spilled some on a snake’s hole. The snake, actually the cursed prince Manasa’s son, appeared and demanded she marry him as penance.

Though frightened, Behula agreed to save her family from the snake’s wrath. She married the snake prince, who transformed into a handsome man at night but remained a serpent by day. Despite the hardship, Behula stayed loyal and kind.

When her husband was later killed by a jealous rival, Behula embarked on a perilous journey to the heavens to plead with the gods for his revival. Her unwavering devotion moved Lord Shiva, who restored her husband’s life and broke the curse, turning him permanently human.

**Moral Lessons:**
1. **Loyalty and Sacrifice** – Behula’s dedication to her husband, despite his serpent form, teaches the value of standing by loved ones in hardship.
2. **Kindness Rewarded** – Her compassion, even toward a snake, led to divine blessings.
3. **Perseverance** – Her relentless effort to revive her husband shows that determination can overcome impossible odds.

The tale highlights that true love and virtue triumph over adversity.
