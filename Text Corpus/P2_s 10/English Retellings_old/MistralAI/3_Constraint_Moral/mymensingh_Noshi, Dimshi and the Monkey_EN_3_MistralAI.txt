**Noshi, Dimshi and the Monkey**

In a Bengali village, two sisters—Noshi (clever) and Dimshi (lazy)—lived with their mother. One day, their mother asked them to fetch firewood. Noshi worked hard, but Dimshi, wanting to avoid labor, tricked a monkey into gathering wood for her by promising him a share of their meal.

When they returned, the mother cooked rice. Dimshi, forgetting her promise, ate her share and left none for the monkey. Furious, the monkey climbed a tree and refused to help again. The next day, Dimshi tried to fool him once more, but the monkey threw down only thorns instead of wood.

Noshi, seeing this, scolded Dimshi: *"Lies and greed bring trouble. Honesty and hard work are better."* The monkey, pleased with Noshi’s fairness, helped her instead.

**Moral:** The tale teaches that laziness and deceit lead to consequences, while honesty and diligence earn respect and rewards. It also highlights the value of keeping promises and treating others fairly.
