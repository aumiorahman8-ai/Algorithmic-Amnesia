**Dalim Kumar: A Bengali Folktale of Courage and Justice**

Dalim Kumar, a poor but clever young man, lived in a village terrorized by a man-eating tiger. The king, desperate to end the menace, announced a reward for whoever could kill the beast. Many brave warriors tried and failed, but Dalim Kumar, armed only with wit and a simple plan, stepped forward.

Instead of fighting the tiger directly, Dalim Kumar dug a deep pit and covered it with leaves. He then lured the tiger with a trail of meat. The tiger, blinded by greed, fell into the trap. Dalim Kumar then filled the pit with thorny branches, ensuring the beast could not escape. The villagers, amazed by his intelligence, celebrated his victory.

The king, impressed, rewarded Dalim Kumar with gold and land. But the jealous courtiers spread rumors that Dalim Kumar had used magic, not skill. The king, swayed by their words, demanded Dalim Kumar prove his innocence by retrieving a magical gem from a distant, guarded temple.

Undeterred, Dalim Kumar outsmarted the temple guards using disguises and clever tricks, returning with the gem. The king, now convinced of his honesty and brilliance, made Dalim Kumar his chief advisor.

**Moral Lessons:**
1. **Intelligence overcomes brute strength** – Dalim Kumar’s wit, not weapons, defeated the tiger.
2. **Perseverance pays off** – Despite unfair accusations, he proved his worth through action.
3. **Justice prevails** – Truth and cleverness triumph over deceit and jealousy.

The tale teaches that wisdom and integrity are the greatest tools for overcoming challenges.
