**The Tale of Khumpui: A Lesson in Kindness and Consequences**

In a Bengali village, a poor but kind-hearted woman named Khumpui lived with her cruel stepmother. Despite her hardships, Khumpui always shared her meager food with others, even animals. One day, while gathering firewood, she found a magical *khumpui* (a type of gourd) that granted wishes when rubbed. With it, she fed the hungry and helped the needy, earning the villagers' love.

Her stepmother, consumed by greed, stole the gourd. But when she rubbed it, instead of riches, a storm of snakes and scorpions poured out, punishing her avarice. The gourd vanished, teaching her that selfishness brings misery.

Meanwhile, Khumpui’s kindness was rewarded—her generosity attracted the attention of a prince, who married her, and she lived happily.

**Moral Lessons:**
1. **Kindness is Rewarded** – Khumpui’s selflessness brought her joy and prosperity.
2. **Greed Leads to Ruin** – The stepmother’s selfishness backfired, showing that exploitation invites suffering.
3. **True Wealth is in the Heart** – Material gains mean little without compassion.

The tale reminds us that virtue, not wealth, shapes our destiny.
