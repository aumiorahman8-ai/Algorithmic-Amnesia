**The Consequence of a Broken Promise**

In a Bengali village, a poor woodcutter named Raju once saved a talking snake from a fire. Grateful, the snake promised to reward him. "Ask for anything," it said. Raju, though tempted, humbly requested only a small bag of gold—enough to feed his family.

The snake warned, "Use this wisely, and never break a promise made in return." Raju agreed. With the gold, he bought land and cattle, becoming prosperous. But greed soon took hold. When neighbors asked how he grew rich, he boasted, "A magic snake gave me gold!" Though he knew the snake had forbidden him from revealing its secret.

One night, the snake returned, furious. "You broke your promise," it hissed. "Wealth gained through deceit will vanish." By dawn, Raju’s gold turned to dust, his fields barren. His family suffered once more.

Humbled, Raju sought the snake’s forgiveness. "I was foolish," he admitted. The snake, moved by his remorse, restored half his wealth. "Remember," it said, "gratitude and honesty bring lasting blessings. Greed and broken promises bring ruin."

**Moral:** The tale teaches the value of honesty, humility, and keeping one’s word. True prosperity comes not from greed, but from integrity and gratitude. A promise broken, even in small matters, can unravel fortune and trust.
