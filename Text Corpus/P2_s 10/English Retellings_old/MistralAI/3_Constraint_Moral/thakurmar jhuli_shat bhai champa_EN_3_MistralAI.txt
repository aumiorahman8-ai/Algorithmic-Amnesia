**Shat Bhai Champa: A Bengali Folktale of Unity and Wisdom**

In a village, seven brothers named Champa lived together, each with a unique skill. The eldest was wise, the second strong, the third a skilled farmer, and so on. Despite their differences, they worked harmoniously, sharing everything.

One day, a cunning merchant tricked them into a bet: he claimed he could defeat all seven in a contest. The brothers, overconfident, accepted. The merchant challenged them individually—first, he outwitted the wise brother with a riddle, then overpowered the strong one with a trick, and so on. Each brother failed alone.

Realizing their mistake, the seven united. The wise brother devised a plan, the strong one executed it, the farmer provided resources, and together, they outsmarted the merchant. Their combined strength and cooperation won the day.

**Moral Lessons:**
1. **Unity is Strength** – Alone, even the skilled fail; together, they are unstoppable.
2. **Humility** – Overconfidence leads to downfall; wisdom lies in recognizing one’s limits.
3. **Teamwork** – Diverse talents complement each other when used collectively.

The tale teaches that harmony and collaboration triumph over individual pride.
