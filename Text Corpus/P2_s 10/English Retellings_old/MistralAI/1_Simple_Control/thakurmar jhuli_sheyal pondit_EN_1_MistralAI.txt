The folktale of **"Sheyal Pondit"** (শেয়াল পণ্ডিত) is a popular Bengali trickster tale featuring a clever fox who outsmarts others with his wit. Here’s a retelling of the story:

---

### **The Tale of Sheyal Pondit (The Clever Fox)**

Once upon a time, in a dense forest, there lived a cunning fox named **Sheyal Pondit** (meaning "Fox the Scholar"). He was known far and wide for his intelligence and quick thinking. One day, as he wandered through the woods, he came across a **tiger** who was roaring in pain.

**"What’s wrong, my friend?"** asked Sheyal Pondit.

The tiger groaned, **"A bone is stuck in my throat! I can’t eat or drink. Please help me!"**

Sheyal Pondit, pretending to be concerned, said, **"Oh dear! That’s a serious problem. But don’t worry—I know a cure. You must eat fresh, warm blood to loosen the bone."**

The tiger, desperate for relief, asked, **"Where can I find fresh blood?"**

The fox grinned slyly. **"I’ll find a goat for you. But first, you must lie down and close your eyes. The blood will work better if you’re calm."**

The tiger, trusting the fox, lay down and shut his eyes. Sheyal Pondit quickly ran to a nearby village and found a **goat**. He whispered to the goat, **"The tiger is in pain. If you go to him, he’ll eat you—but I’ll save you!"**

The frightened goat agreed. Sheyal Pondit led the goat to the tiger and said, **"Here’s your medicine!"** Then, just as the tiger was about to pounce, the fox shouted, **"Wait! The blood must be warm. Let me check first!"**

He pretended to examine the goat and then said, **"This goat is too skinny. I’ll find a fatter one!"** He took the goat away and returned with another, repeating the same trick. Each time, he "saved" the goat and sent it back to the village.

After several attempts, the tiger grew impatient. **"Enough! Just bring me a goat and let me eat!"**

Sheyal Pondit, still pretending to help, said, **"Alright, but first, let me remove the bone myself."** He reached into the tiger’s mouth, pulled out the bone, and threw it away. **"There! Now you’re fine!"**

The tiger, now free from pain, realized he had been tricked. **"You fooled me! I’ll eat you instead!"** he roared.

But Sheyal Pondit was already running away, laughing. **"You’re too slow, my friend! Next time, don’t trust a fox!"**

And with that, the clever fox escaped, leaving the tiger humiliated and hungry.

---

### **Moral of the Story:**
The tale teaches that **cleverness can outsmart brute strength**, and one should always be cautious of those who seem too helpful. Sheyal Pondit’s wit saves him (and the goats) from the tiger’s claws.
