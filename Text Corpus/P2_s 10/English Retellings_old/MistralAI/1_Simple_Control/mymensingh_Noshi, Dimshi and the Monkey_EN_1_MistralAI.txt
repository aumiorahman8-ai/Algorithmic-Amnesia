### **Noshi, Dimshi and the Monkey**

Once upon a time, in a small village in Bengal, there lived two sisters—**Noshi** and **Dimshi**. Noshi was kind, hardworking, and always helped others, while Dimshi was lazy, selfish, and often jealous of her sister.

One day, their mother asked them to fetch water from the river. Noshi quickly filled her pitcher and returned home, but Dimshi took her time, playing by the riverbank. As she was about to leave, she saw a **monkey** sitting on a tree, eating a banana.

Dimshi, being greedy, thought, *"If I give the monkey some food, maybe he’ll give me something in return!"* So, she took out a piece of bread from her basket and offered it to the monkey. The monkey happily ate it and then, to her surprise, gave her a **golden necklace** in return!

Excited, Dimshi ran home and showed the necklace to her mother and Noshi. Noshi was happy for her sister, but Dimshi, being selfish, didn’t share the story of how she got it.

The next day, Noshi went to the river again. The monkey appeared and asked her for food. Noshi, being kind, gave him some rice. The monkey, pleased, gave her a **golden bangle** in return.

When Dimshi saw Noshi’s bangle, she became furious. *"Why did the monkey give her something too?"* she thought. The next day, she went back to the river, but this time, she didn’t bring any food. Instead, she shouted at the monkey, *"Give me more gold, or I’ll throw stones at you!"*

The monkey, angered by her greed, climbed down and **snatched the necklace and bangle** from her. Then, he threw them into the river and vanished.

Dimshi cried and ran home, but it was too late. The monkey had punished her for her greed and selfishness.

From that day on, Dimshi learned her lesson and tried to be kind like Noshi. And the two sisters lived happily, always sharing what they had.

**Moral of the Story:** *Kindness is always rewarded, but greed leads to loss.*
