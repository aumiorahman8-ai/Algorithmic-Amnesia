### **The Consequence of a Broken Promise**

Long ago, in a small village in Bengal, there lived a poor but kind-hearted farmer named **Ramu**. One day, while plowing his field, his plow struck something hard. Digging deeper, he discovered a **golden pot** filled with coins.

Overjoyed, Ramu decided to take the pot home. But as he lifted it, a **mysterious voice** echoed:

*"Take this treasure, but remember—you must never tell anyone how you found it. If you break this promise, you will lose everything!"*

Ramu, trembling with fear and excitement, agreed. He took the pot home and hid it carefully. With the gold, he bought fertile land, built a fine house, and lived comfortably. His wife, **Laxmi**, was delighted but curious.

"Where did this wealth come from, husband?" she asked.

Ramu, remembering the warning, refused to tell her. But Laxmi grew suspicious. She nagged and pleaded until, unable to bear her persistence, Ramu finally confessed:

*"I found a golden pot in the field, but I was warned never to tell anyone!"*

The moment the words left his mouth, a **terrible storm** erupted. The skies darkened, and a **fierce wind** howled through the village. The golden pot **vanished** from its hiding place, and Ramu’s wealth turned to dust. His fields withered, his house crumbled, and he was left poorer than before.

Laxmi wept in regret, but it was too late. The **spirit of the treasure** had punished Ramu for breaking his promise.

From that day on, Ramu wandered the village, warning others:

*"Never break a promise, for honesty is the greatest wealth of all."*

And so, the tale of **Ramu’s broken promise** became a lesson for generations—**trust, once lost, is hard to regain, and deceit brings only ruin.**

---

### **Moral of the Story:**
- **A promise is sacred; breaking it invites misfortune.**
- **Greed and deceit lead to downfall, while honesty brings true prosperity.**
