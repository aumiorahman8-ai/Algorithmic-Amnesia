### **Kolaboti Rajkonna (The Pumpkin Princess)**

Once upon a time, in a distant kingdom, there lived a kind and beautiful princess named Kolaboti. She was beloved by her people, but her stepmother, the queen, was wicked and jealous of her. The queen wanted her own daughter to inherit the throne, so she plotted to get rid of Kolaboti.

One day, the queen tricked Kolaboti into eating a magical fruit that turned her into a **giant pumpkin (kolaboti in Bengali)**. The queen then threw the pumpkin into a deep well, hoping no one would ever find her.

Years passed, and the kingdom fell into despair under the cruel rule of the queen and her daughter. Meanwhile, a poor but brave young man named **Raju** lived nearby. One day, while fetching water from the well, he heard a soft voice calling for help. To his surprise, the voice came from the pumpkin!

Raju took the pumpkin home and, with great care, nurtured it. One night, as he slept, the pumpkin transformed back into the beautiful Princess Kolaboti. She revealed her true identity and told Raju about the queen’s treachery.

With Raju’s help, Kolaboti returned to the palace and exposed the queen’s evil deeds. The people rejoiced, and the queen was banished. Kolaboti regained her throne, and in gratitude, she married Raju, who became the prince consort.

From then on, the kingdom prospered under their wise and just rule, and the tale of **Kolaboti Rajkonna** was told for generations as a story of kindness, courage, and justice.

---

### **Moral of the Story:**
- **Goodness triumphs over evil.**
- **True love and kindness can break even the strongest curses.**
