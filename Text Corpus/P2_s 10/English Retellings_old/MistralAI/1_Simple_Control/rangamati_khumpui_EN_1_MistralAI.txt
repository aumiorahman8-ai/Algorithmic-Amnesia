The folktale of **Khumpui** is a lesser-known but enchanting story from the **Chakma community** of Bangladesh and parts of Northeast India. It is a tale of love, magic, and the consequences of breaking promises. Here’s a retelling of the story:

---

### **The Tale of Khumpui**

Long ago, in a lush green village nestled among the hills, there lived a beautiful young woman named **Khumpui**. She was known far and wide for her kindness, grace, and mesmerizing voice. Many young men admired her, but none could win her heart.

One day, a handsome and mysterious young man named **Rupai** arrived in the village. He was unlike anyone Khumpui had ever met—his eyes sparkled with wisdom, and his words carried the weight of ancient secrets. The two fell deeply in love, and soon, they decided to marry.

Before the wedding, Rupai revealed a secret to Khumpui: *"I am not an ordinary man. I am a spirit of the forest, bound by the laws of nature. If you ever break your promise to me, I will vanish forever."*

Khumpui, filled with love and trust, promised, *"I will never betray you, no matter what."*

For many years, they lived happily. Rupai would often disappear into the forest, returning with rare fruits, herbs, and stories of the unseen world. Khumpui, though curious, never questioned him.

One day, a group of villagers grew suspicious of Rupai. They whispered that he was not human but a **forest spirit** who had enchanted Khumpui. Fearing for her safety, they warned her: *"He is not one of us. You must leave him before it’s too late."*

At first, Khumpui refused to believe them, but doubt crept into her heart. One evening, as Rupai slept, she couldn’t resist the temptation to test his secret. She took a sharp knife and gently pricked his finger. To her horror, instead of blood, **sap oozed out**—proof that he was indeed a spirit of the trees.

Tears filled her eyes as she realized what she had done. Rupai awoke instantly, his face filled with sorrow. *"You broke your promise,"* he said softly. *"Now, I must return to the forest, and you will never see me again."*

Before Khumpui could apologize, Rupai dissolved into a swirl of leaves and mist, vanishing into the wilderness. Heartbroken, Khumpui searched for him for years, calling his name in the depths of the forest, but he was gone forever.

Some say that on quiet nights, if you listen closely near the old trees, you can still hear Khumpui’s sorrowful voice singing, waiting for her lost love to return.

---

### **Moral of the Story**
The tale of **Khumpui** teaches the importance of **trust and keeping promises**. Love, once broken by doubt, may never be regained. It also reflects the deep connection between humans and nature in Chakma folklore, where spirits and mortals often intertwine.
