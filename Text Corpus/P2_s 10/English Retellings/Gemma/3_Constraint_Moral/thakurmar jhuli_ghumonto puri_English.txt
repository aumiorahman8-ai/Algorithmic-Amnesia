## The Tale of Ghumonto Puri

Ghumonto Puri was a magical city, appearing and disappearing at will, famed for its delicious, eternally-fresh *puri* (fried bread). A poor woodcutter, Ramu, heard tales of it and, driven by hunger, relentlessly searched for years. Finally, guided by a kind old woman, he found the city.

He was warned: eat only what you need, and never be greedy. Ghumonto Puri provides for the truly hungry, but punishes avarice. Ramu, initially mindful, enjoyed the *puri*. But seeing others take more, he began to hoard, stuffing his bag and clothes, convinced he could sell it for a fortune.

Suddenly, the city vanished! Ramu found himself alone in the forest, his bag filled not with *puri*, but with stones. He’d lost everything, even the simple meal he’d already enjoyed. 

Dejected, he returned home, realizing his mistake. He’d been blinded by greed, forgetting the city’s gift was meant for sustenance, not profit. 

The tale of Ghumonto Puri teaches us that **contentment is key**.  It warns against **greed and the desire for more than we need**. True blessings are lost when we prioritize profit over gratitude and mindful consumption.  The city appears only to those with genuine need, and disappears when selfishness takes root.
