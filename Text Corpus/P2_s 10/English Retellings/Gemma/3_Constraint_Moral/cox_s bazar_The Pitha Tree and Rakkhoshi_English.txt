Long ago, a poor woodcutter named Ramu lived with his wife, Lali. They were blessed with a magical pitha (sweet cake) tree in their yard, providing them with endless food. However, a fearsome Rakkhoshi (female demon), envious of their fortune, began stealing the pithas nightly.

Ramu tried everything – traps, guards – but the Rakkhoshi was too clever. Finally, Lali suggested a different approach. Instead of protecting the pithas, they began leaving out a particularly delicious one, specifically for the Rakkhoshi, with a small, polite note asking her to eat her fill.

The Rakkhoshi, surprised by their kindness, initially suspected a trick. But night after night, the offering continued, accompanied by gentle words. Slowly, her heart softened. She revealed she stole out of loneliness and hunger. 

Ramu and Lali began sharing their abundance with her, and the Rakkhoshi, in turn, protected their home. The pitha tree flourished even more.

The tale of the Pitha Tree and Rakkhoshi teaches us that **kindness and empathy can conquer even the most fearsome of beings.** It demonstrates that **responding to negativity with generosity can disarm hostility.**  Instead of fighting evil with force, offering understanding and compassion can lead to unexpected friendship and lasting peace.  It also highlights the power of **sharing and abundance** – giving doesn't diminish what you have, it can actually increase it.
