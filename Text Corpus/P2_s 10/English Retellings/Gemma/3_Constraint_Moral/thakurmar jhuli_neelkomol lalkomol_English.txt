## Neelkomol & Lalkomol: A Tale of Inner Beauty

Neelkomol (Blue Lotus) and Lalkomol (Red Lotus) were twin sisters, famed throughout the kingdom for their beauty. Lalkomol was outwardly stunning, attracting suitors with her radiant face. Neelkomol, however, was considered plain, her beauty hidden within a darker complexion.

A king, seeking a bride, declared a contest: whoever could weave the finest cloth would win his hand. Lalkomol, relying on her looks, used inferior materials, expecting her beauty to compensate. Neelkomol, despite her perceived lack of beauty, diligently worked with the best silk, weaving a cloth of exceptional quality and intricate design.

The king, unimpressed by Lalkomol’s flashy but flawed work, was captivated by Neelkomol’s masterpiece. He chose Neelkomol, recognizing true worth lay not in superficial appearance, but in dedication, skill, and inner quality. 

Lalkomol, humbled, realized her mistake. The tale of Neelkomol and Lalkomol teaches us that **true beauty is not skin deep.** It emphasizes the importance of **cultivating inner qualities like diligence, humility, and skill** over relying on outward appearances. It’s a reminder that **genuine worth is earned through effort and character**, and that judging solely on looks can lead to misjudgment and regret.
