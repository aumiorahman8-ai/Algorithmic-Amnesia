Once, two brothers, Dhanu and Manu, were famed polo players, but consumed by pride. They never spoke, communicating only through skillful plays, each secretly believing himself the better. This “silent quarrel” poisoned their relationship, turning every victory into a source of hidden resentment.

A cunning king, observing their strained dynamic, devised a test. He ordered a polo field built over a hidden trap – a deep pit disguised with mats. He announced a tournament, promising a grand prize. Dhanu and Manu, driven by ego, played fiercely, ignoring each other’s signals. 

Predictably, their lack of communication led to disaster. Both, attempting a simultaneous strike, fell into the pit! Humiliated and bruised, they finally spoke, realizing their pride had blinded them. They confessed their silent rivalry and the king revealed his lesson.

The king explained that true strength lies not in individual brilliance, but in cooperation and humility. He awarded them the prize *together*, emphasizing that a team’s success depends on open communication and respect. 

The tale of Dhanu and Manu teaches us that **pride breeds isolation and hinders collaboration.**  **Open communication and humility are essential for success, both in games and in life.**  A silent quarrel, left unchecked, can lead to a fall for everyone involved.
