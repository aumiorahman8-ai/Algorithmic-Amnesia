## Shat Bhai Champa: A Tale of Unity & Kindness

Once upon a time, a king lamented having no sons. Finally, a goddess granted him seven sons, born from a single *champa* flower – hence, ‘Shat Bhai Champa’ (Seven Brothers Champa). Though incredibly strong and skilled, the brothers were constantly bickering, their individual pride hindering any collective achievement.

The king, disheartened, tasked them with a seemingly impossible feat: bringing him the feather of a magical bird, the *khonajon*. Each brother set off alone, facing challenges they couldn’t overcome due to their selfishness. 

Finally, the youngest, recognizing their past failures, sought out his brothers. He convinced them to work *together*. Pooling their skills – one could climb, another swim, another charm animals – they successfully retrieved the feather.

The king, overjoyed, realized the true treasure wasn't the feather, but his sons’ newfound unity. He declared that only together could they rule the kingdom.

**The moral of Shat Bhai Champa is clear: individual strength is valuable, but collective effort and brotherly love are far more powerful.** The tale emphasizes the importance of overcoming pride, embracing cooperation, and recognizing that true success lies in unity and kindness towards one another. It’s a timeless lesson about the strength found in family and community.
