Once upon a time, a beautiful Shol fish lived in a pond, renowned for its shimmering scales and sweet song. A cunning jackal, hearing the song, devised a plan. He praised the Shol’s voice, claiming the river was longing to hear it. Flattered, the Shol agreed to be carried to the river, believing in the jackal’s false flattery.

The jackal, however, had no intention of a river concert. He carried the Shol further and further, stopping to enjoy a delicious meal – the Shol itself! As the Shol lay dying, it lamented its foolish vanity, realizing it traded its safe pond for empty praise.

A passing woodcutter heard the Shol’s final words and, witnessing the jackal’s cruelty, chased him away. He then warned others about the dangers of believing flattering words.

The tale of the Shol fish teaches a vital lesson: **beware of excessive pride and empty flattery.** The Shol’s vanity blinded it to the jackal’s true intentions. It highlights the importance of **critical thinking and trusting your instincts**, rather than seeking validation from those who may have ulterior motives. True worth isn't found in the praise of others, but in recognizing and valuing your own safety and well-being.
