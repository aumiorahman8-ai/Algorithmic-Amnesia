Noshi and Dimshi were two sisters, famed for their beauty but notorious for their laziness. They refused to do any housework, leaving their poor mother exhausted. One day, their mother, exasperated, sent them to the forest to collect firewood, warning them to beware of the mischievous monkey.

The sisters dawdled, eventually finding a tree laden with ripe mangoes. Ignoring their task, they began greedily eating the fruit. A clever monkey appeared and, seeing their selfishness, snatched their basket. He promised to return it *if* they could spin straw into gold – an impossible task!

Desperate, they pleaded with the monkey, who eventually agreed to help, but only if they promised to work hard and help their mother from then on. He magically spun the straw, returning the basket filled with gold. 

Noshi and Dimshi, humbled and finally understanding the value of hard work, kept their promise. They diligently helped their mother, realizing that laziness and greed lead to trouble, while effort and responsibility bring rewards. 

The tale of Noshi, Dimshi and the Monkey teaches us that **hard work is essential, greed is detrimental, and keeping one's promises is vital for a fulfilling life.** It emphasizes the importance of contributing to the family and avoiding shortcuts that rely on trickery or laziness.
