Suanlo Suanlo was a boastful woodcutter, famed for his cleverness – or so he thought. He constantly bragged about outsmarting a tiger, claiming he’d tricked the beast into letting him go by praising its stripes as beautiful, unlike any other tiger’s. The tiger, flattered, released him.

Word reached the tiger, who was furious to discover Suanlo had been mocking him, telling everyone his stripes were unique because they were *ugly* on all other tigers! Enraged, the tiger disguised himself as a travelling merchant and visited Suanlo’s village. He offered Suanlo a job carrying a heavy chest, promising great reward. 

Unbeknownst to Suanlo, the chest contained the tiger himself! As they journeyed deep into the forest, the tiger revealed his true form, intending to punish Suanlo for his deceit. However, Suanlo, terrified, genuinely apologized, admitting his boastfulness and lies. 

Impressed by Suanlo’s sincere remorse, the tiger spared him, but warned him: **Honesty and humility are far more valuable than clever lies.** Suanlo returned home a changed man, learning that bragging and disrespect, even towards powerful creatures, always come with a price. The tale teaches us the importance of truthfulness, respect for others, and the dangers of vanity.
