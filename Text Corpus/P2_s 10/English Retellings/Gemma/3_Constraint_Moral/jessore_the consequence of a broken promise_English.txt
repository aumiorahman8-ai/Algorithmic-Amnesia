Long ago, a poor woodcutter, Ramu, promised a magical golden deer he’d rescued from a hunter that he’d never reveal its true nature. The deer, in return, granted Ramu the ability to have any wish fulfilled simply by saying "Seshe!" (meaning "Finished!").

Ramu initially used his power wisely, providing for his family. However, greed crept in. When the local king offered a vast reward for such a deer, Ramu, forgetting his promise, boasted of his possession and the deer’s magic. 

Immediately, the deer vanished, and Ramu lost his power. The king, furious at being tricked, imprisoned Ramu. He realized his folly – the fleeting benefit of wealth gained through dishonesty was nothing compared to the lasting value of trust and a kept promise.

Ramu languished in jail, learning a harsh lesson. He understood that true wealth lies not in magical gifts, but in integrity. The tale teaches that breaking a promise, even to a creature seemingly less powerful, carries severe consequences. It emphasizes the importance of honesty, gratitude, and the understanding that short-term gain achieved through deceit ultimately leads to loss and suffering.
