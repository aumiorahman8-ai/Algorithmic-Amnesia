## Brahman & Brahmani: A Tale of Greed & Contentment

Brahman and Brahmani were a poor but content couple. One day, a divine being disguised as a beggar offered Brahman a magical pot. It provided a limitless supply of delicious food – but only as long as they didn’t tell anyone. 

Initially overjoyed, they lived comfortably. However, Brahman, driven by greed, confided in his wife about the pot’s secret. Brahmani, unable to contain her excitement, blurted it out to a neighbor. 

Immediately, the pot stopped working. Desperate, they tried everything, but the magic was gone. They returned to their former poverty, realizing their mistake.

A wise old man explained the lesson: true happiness lies in contentment and discretion. The gift wasn't about endless food, but about appreciating what they *had* and the virtue of keeping blessings private. Sharing joy is good, but boasting about miraculous fortune invites loss. 

The tale of Brahman and Brahmani warns against the dangers of greed, the importance of humility, and the power of contentment. It emphasizes that true wealth isn't material, but a peaceful heart and a grateful spirit.
