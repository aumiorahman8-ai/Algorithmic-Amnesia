Once upon a time, a beautiful princess was cursed to transform into a python each night. Only a truly selfless act could break the spell. Four brothers, each renowned for a skill – archery, horse riding, music, and storytelling – sought to win her hand, unaware of her affliction.

Each brother attempted to impress the princess, boasting of their talents. The first three offered displays of skill, hoping to earn her favor. However, the youngest, a humble storyteller, noticed the princess’s sadness and, instead of performing, simply sat and listened to her woes. He offered comfort and genuine empathy, asking nothing in return.

As he listened, the python appeared. Without fear, he continued to speak kindly, sharing stories to soothe the creature. His selfless compassion, his focus on *her* well-being, broke the curse. The princess returned to her human form, choosing the youngest brother as her husband.

The tale teaches that true worth isn't found in boasting or skill, but in **empathy, kindness, and selfless service**. The princess wasn't seeking a performer, but someone who could see *beyond* her exterior and offer genuine connection. It highlights that **listening and understanding are often more powerful than grand gestures**, and that **true love prioritizes the well-being of another.**
