## The Tale of Khumpui: The Hollow Tree Spirit

The story of Khumpui is a chilling tale told in the villages of Sylhet and surrounding areas of Bangladesh and India, a warning about greed, disrespect for nature, and the dangers lurking in the unseen world. It centers around a spirit that dwells within a specific type of old, hollow tree – the Khumpui tree.

Long ago, in a small, impoverished village, lived a young man named Rahim. He was a hardworking farmer, but perpetually unlucky. Despite toiling from dawn till dusk, his harvests were meager, and his family often went hungry. He watched his neighbors prosper, and a seed of envy began to grow in his heart.

One day, while clearing land for his fields, Rahim stumbled upon a massive, ancient tree. It was unlike any he’d seen before – incredibly tall, with a vast, hollow trunk. The villagers warned him about this tree. They called it a Khumpui tree, and said it was the home of a powerful, mischievous spirit also named Khumpui. They said the spirit granted wishes, but at a terrible price.  It demanded respect, and those who were greedy or disrespectful would face dire consequences.

Rahim, desperate to escape his poverty, scoffed at the warnings. He thought the stories were just old wives' tales meant to scare children. He believed he was clever enough to outsmart the spirit. He decided he would ask Khumpui for wealth, but wouldn't give anything substantial in return.

He approached the tree at night, a time when the spirit was said to be most active. He called out, "Khumpui! Khumpui! I know you live within this tree. I am a poor man, and I ask for your help. Grant me wealth, and I will offer you… a handful of rice!"

A chilling wind rustled through the leaves, and a voice, seemingly emanating from the hollow of the tree, replied, "So, you seek my favor, yet offer so little? Very well. I will grant your wish. But remember, everything has a price."

Rahim, overjoyed, dismissed the warning. Soon after, his fortunes began to change. He found a hidden treasure, his crops flourished, and he became the wealthiest man in the village. He built a grand house, wore fine clothes, and ate the best food. He completely forgot his promise and the warning of the spirit.

He became arrogant and boastful, flaunting his wealth and looking down upon those who were still struggling. He even started cutting down trees in the forest, disregarding the pleas of the villagers who revered the natural world. 

Khumpui, observing Rahim’s greed and disrespect, grew furious.  One night, as Rahim slept soundly in his opulent bed, a strange illness fell upon him. He began to swell, growing larger and larger with each passing hour.  His body became bloated and distorted, resembling… a hollow tree trunk. 

The village doctor was baffled. No medicine could help.  As Rahim continued to swell, he realized with horror what was happening. He was being transformed into a Khumpui tree himself!

He cried out in despair, begging for forgiveness. He remembered his paltry offering and his disrespect for the spirit and the forest. But it was too late.  

By morning, Rahim was gone. In his place stood a massive, hollow tree, identical to the one he had initially encountered.  The villagers, witnessing this terrifying transformation, understood the price of greed and disrespect. 

The new Khumpui tree stood as a stark reminder:  **Never underestimate the power of the spirits, and always respect the gifts of nature.  A small offering given with sincerity is worth more than a mountain of riches offered with a selfish heart.**



To this day, villagers avoid the Khumpui trees, offering small tokens of respect – flowers, rice, or incense – to appease the spirit within, and to ensure they don't suffer the same fate as Rahim. The tale of Khumpui serves as a powerful cautionary story, woven into the fabric of Bengali folklore.
