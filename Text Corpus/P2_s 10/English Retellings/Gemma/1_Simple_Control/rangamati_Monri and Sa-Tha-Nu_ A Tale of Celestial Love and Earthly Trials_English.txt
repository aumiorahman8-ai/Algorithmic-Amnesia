## Monri and Sa-Tha-Nu: A Tale of Celestial Love and Earthly Trials

The story of Monri and Sa-Tha-Nu is a beloved Bengali folktale, a poignant tale of a celestial being falling in love with a mortal woman, and the trials they face to be together. It’s a story woven with magic, hardship, and the enduring power of love.

Long ago, in a village nestled beside a vast, shimmering river, lived a beautiful young woman named Monri. She was known for her kindness, her gentle spirit, and a voice that could charm the birds from the trees. Every evening, she would sing by the riverbank, her melodies echoing through the fields.

Unbeknownst to Monri, her songs reached the ears of Sa-Tha-Nu, a handsome prince from the celestial realm. He wasn't a prince of gold and jewels, but of light and stars, a being of pure energy and grace. Captivated by Monri’s voice and the purity of her heart, Sa-Tha-Nu descended to Earth, disguising himself as a young man.

He met Monri by the river, drawn to her like a moth to a flame. He was immediately smitten, and Monri, in turn, found herself falling for the mysterious stranger with eyes that held the light of the heavens. They fell deeply in love, spending their days in quiet companionship, sharing stories and songs. Sa-Tha-Nu, however, kept his true identity a secret, fearing Monri wouldn't accept him if she knew he was a celestial being.

Their happiness was short-lived. Monri’s family, noticing her growing affection for the stranger, arranged for her to marry a wealthy, but cruel, landowner. Monri was heartbroken, but bound by duty and respect for her family, she reluctantly agreed. 

Sa-Tha-Nu, witnessing Monri’s despair, finally revealed his true nature. He told her of his celestial home, of his love for her songs, and of his heartbreak at seeing her forced into a loveless marriage. Monri was astonished, but her love for him was stronger than her fear.

Together, they devised a plan. Sa-Tha-Nu, using his celestial powers, created a magical boat, a *nouka* made of moonlight and stardust. He promised to take Monri away to his celestial realm, where they could be together forever. 

However, the journey wasn't easy. The cruel landowner, enraged by Monri’s rejection, pursued them, determined to reclaim his bride. He enlisted the help of a powerful sorcerer who conjured storms and monstrous creatures to sink the magical boat.

Sa-Tha-Nu fought valiantly, using his celestial powers to deflect the sorcerer’s attacks. But the storms were relentless, and the boat began to falter.  Monri, seeing Sa-Tha-Nu weakening, began to sing. Her voice, filled with love and desperation, was so powerful that it calmed the raging seas and weakened the sorcerer’s spells. 

But the sorcerer, in a final act of malice, cast a spell that transformed Monri into a pair of beautiful, singing birds – the *Monri-pakkha* (Monri birds).  Sa-Tha-Nu was devastated. He couldn't bear to leave her in this form, but he also couldn't undo the sorcerer's powerful magic.

Instead of taking her to the celestial realm, Sa-Tha-Nu decided to stay on Earth with Monri, forever bound to her in her bird form. He transformed himself into a beautiful, flowering tree, the *Sa-Tha-Nu-gach* (Sa-Tha-Nu tree), providing shelter and a place for the Monri birds to nest.

And so, they remained, a testament to their enduring love. The Monri birds sing sweetly amongst the branches of the Sa-Tha-Nu tree, their melodies echoing the love story that transcends worlds.  

The tale of Monri and Sa-Tha-Nu is a reminder that true love knows no boundaries, and that even in the face of adversity, love can find a way to endure, even if it means sacrificing everything for the sake of the beloved.  It’s a story that continues to be told and retold in Bengal, a beautiful and melancholic ballad of celestial love and earthly trials.
