A husband and wife were in the midst of a two-day silence following a quarrel. The wife noticed villagers heading to the wetlands with polo traps to fish and wanted her husband to join them. Unable to speak to him directly due to their argument, she initiated a strange, indirect conversation. 

She asked who would go fishing *for her*, prompting him to explain he hadn’t eaten. She then pointed out the rice and curry were ready, but he complained the rice was a mixed mess of long and short grains. Finally, the wife told him to simply eat the rice despite his complaints, suggesting he “mould a mouthful atop his insults and swallow it whole.” The exchange was clearly a veiled attempt to get him to go fishing and a continuation of their underlying disagreement.
