A man remarried after his first wife died, needing someone to manage his household. He cautioned his new wife on their wedding night to treat his son from his first marriage with kindness and ensure he came to no harm. The wife promised to obey.

She then had three sons of her own, but favored her stepson, carrying him while her own children remained on the floor. She prioritized his education, sending him to school while her sons worked the fields. Eventually, the stepson abandoned his studies to pursue marriage, which his stepmother arranged. 

After marrying and having children, the stepson began selling off his portion of the family land, which his half-brothers purchased. This continued until he was left with nothing. The man, witnessing this, finally confronted his wife, stating she had not shown his son the affection he had requested.
