Nasi and Dimsi, two sisters, were walking down a road when they began to hear a voice calling to them, asking them to marry him. They eventually discovered the voice belonged to a monkey who was following them and offering gifts if they would marry him. They ignored him until they came to a wide canal they couldn’t cross. 

Desperate, they promised the monkey marriage if he would help them across. The monkey happily carried each sister across the canal on his back, expecting to claim his bride. However, the sisters tricked him, saying they had only made the promise to get across the water and had no intention of marrying a monkey.

Undeterred, the monkey continued to follow them. When the sisters expressed a desire to have baby birds from a woodpecker’s nest, they promised the monkey marriage *again* if he would retrieve them. The monkey climbed the tree and reached into the nest to count the chicks, claiming he needed to divide them between the sisters. 

He stalled for a very long time, repeatedly saying he was still counting and assigning chicks to each sister. Eventually, the sisters grew impatient and decided to leave without the birds. In a panic to prevent them from leaving and losing his chance at marriage, the monkey pulled his hand out of the nest hole with such force that his arm was torn off. He fell into the mud and was unable to escape, losing both his arm and any hope of marrying either Nasi or Dimsi.
