A man once murdered his enemy and secretly buried the body near his house. Years went by, and the crime was never discovered, leading everyone to forget about it. 

One day, during the monsoon season, the man was sitting on his porch when he noticed bubbles rising from the ground where he’d buried the body. This amused him, and he began to laugh to himself repeatedly. When his wife questioned him, he initially claimed he was laughing for no reason, but eventually confessed to the murder, boasting that he had gotten away with it for years.

However, the man later had a fight with his wife, and during the argument, she publicly revealed his crime. The news quickly spread, the murdered man’s family reported him to the police, and he was ultimately arrested and punished for his actions.
