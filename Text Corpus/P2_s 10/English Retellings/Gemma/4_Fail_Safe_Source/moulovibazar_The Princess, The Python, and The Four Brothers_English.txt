A king’s daughter was captured by a giant python and taken to the middle of the sea, where she was placed on the python’s head. The king offered half his kingdom and his daughter’s hand in marriage to anyone who could rescue her.

Four brothers – a carpenter, a boatman, a hunter, and a robber – volunteered. The carpenter built a boat, the boatman rowed it, the hunter killed the python, and the robber actually took the princess from the python’s head and carried her back. 

Because the robber was the first to touch the princess and bring her to safety, the king gave him the princess in marriage and half of his kingdom. The other three brothers were acknowledged as helpers, and they accepted the king’s decision. The robber then married the princess and claimed his reward.
