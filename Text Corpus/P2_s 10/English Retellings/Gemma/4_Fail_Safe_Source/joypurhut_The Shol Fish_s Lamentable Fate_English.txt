During the monsoon season, a young boy caught a Shol fish with his fishing hook. He took the fish home to his mother, who began to clean it – scraping off its scales and cutting its gills. While she was away, a kite saw the fish and, wanting it for itself, snatched it up and flew away. However, the kite dropped the fish into a pond. 

Now free, the Shol fish saw other boys fishing and spoke to them, recounting its terrible ordeal. It described being caught repeatedly, nearly losing its head in a fish trap, having its gills cut, and being carried away by the kite across a great distance. It then challenged the boys to understand the pain of being caught on a hook, suggesting they experience it for themselves.
