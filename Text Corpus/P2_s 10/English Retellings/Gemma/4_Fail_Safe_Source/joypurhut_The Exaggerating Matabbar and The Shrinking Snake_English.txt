A village headman died, and his son, eager for the power his father held, tried to take his place. However, the son was unintelligent and relied on his nephew, who simply flattered him. The new headman constantly tried to show his authority and told many unbelievable stories. 

His nephew warned him that these stories would reveal his lack of intelligence, so they agreed the nephew would say "hmm" whenever the headman exaggerated. During a village council meeting, the headman began telling a story about a giant snake – initially claiming it was 100 cubits long. When challenged, and after repeated "hmm" signals from his nephew as he lowered the snake’s size to 90, 80, and then 70 cubits, he finally insisted the snake was at least 60 cubits long. 

This stubbornness and inability to admit his exaggeration caused everyone to laugh and criticize him. Humiliated, the headman left the meeting and was never again seen at village councils or in judgment.
