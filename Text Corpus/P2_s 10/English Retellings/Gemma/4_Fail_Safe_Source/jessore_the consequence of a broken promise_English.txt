Here's a retelling of the story, based *solely* on the provided text:

A custom existed at where the Mathabhanga river flows, requiring merchants to offer a portion of their goods to the goddess. One merchant refused to offer anything and insulted the goddess, resulting in his boat sinking. Attempts to recover the boat failed – divers couldn’t find the riverbed, no matter how much rope they used.

Eventually, a brave man volunteered to dive. He discovered a golden boat where two fairies were pulling on all the rope that had been let down, creating a huge pile. The fairies agreed to return the merchant’s boat if the diver promised to tell no one about what he had seen. They directed him to find the boat at the Kazis' ghat.

The boat *was* found at the Kazis' ghat, but the man broke his promise and told his wife about the fairies and the golden boat. As a result, he immediately vomited blood and died.
