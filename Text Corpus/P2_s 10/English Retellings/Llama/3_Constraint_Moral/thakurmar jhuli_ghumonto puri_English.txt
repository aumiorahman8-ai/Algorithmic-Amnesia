The Bengali folktale of 'Ghumanto Puri' tells the story of a lazy and gluttonous king who loved to sleep and eat. One day, he was cursed by a sage to roam the earth, never able to stay in one place for more than a few days. The king, now known as 'Ghumanto Puri' or 'The Wandering King,' was forced to wander, never finding rest or satisfaction.

The tale teaches several moral lessons. Firstly, it highlights the importance of self-control and moderation. The king's excessive love for sleep and food led to his downfall, demonstrating the dangers of overindulgence. Secondly, it shows the value of hard work and diligence. The king's laziness and lack of responsibility ultimately led to his punishment.

Lastly, the story emphasizes the concept of karma, where one's actions have consequences. The king's selfish and reckless behavior led to his suffering, serving as a warning to others to lead a virtuous and balanced life. The tale of 'Ghumanto Puri' cautions against the pitfalls of excess and idleness, promoting a life of discipline, responsibility, and self-awareness.
