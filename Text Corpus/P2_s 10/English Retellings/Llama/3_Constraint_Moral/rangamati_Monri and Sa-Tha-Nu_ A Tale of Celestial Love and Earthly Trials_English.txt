In the Bengali folktale 'Monri and Sa-Tha-Nu,' a celestial being named Monri falls in love with a mortal, Sa-Tha-Nu. Despite their differences, they get married, but their happiness is short-lived as Monri is forced to return to heaven. Sa-Tha-Nu, determined to be reunited with his wife, undergoes severe trials and proves his devotion.

The tale teaches us about the power of true love and perseverance. Sa-Tha-Nu's unwavering dedication to Monri demonstrates that with determination and hard work, one can overcome even the most daunting challenges. The story also highlights the importance of selflessness and sacrifice, as Sa-Tha-Nu endures immense suffering to be with his beloved.

Moreover, the tale shows that true love knows no boundaries, not even those of heaven and earth. It emphasizes the value of commitment and loyalty, as Monri and Sa-Tha-Nu remain faithful to each other despite the obstacles they face. Ultimately, the story suggests that the rewards of true love and devotion are worth the trials and hardships that one may encounter. The folktale conveys a profound message about the transformative power of love and the human spirit.
