"Shonar Kati Rupar Kati" is a Bengali folktale that teaches valuable lessons about the consequences of greed and the importance of contentment. The story revolves around a poor farmer who discovers a magical golden axe and a silver axe. The golden axe has the power to grant any wish, but with a twist: it takes something precious in return. The silver axe, on the other hand, fulfills wishes without demanding anything in exchange, but only if the wish is selfless.

As the farmer uses the golden axe, he becomes increasingly greedy, wishing for more and more wealth, but losing something precious each time. Eventually, he loses everything that truly matters to him. In contrast, when he uses the silver axe to make selfless wishes, he finds happiness and fulfillment.

The tale conveys the moral that greed and selfishness can lead to loss and suffering, while selflessness and contentment can bring true happiness. It also highlights the importance of being mindful of the consequences of one's actions and the value of appreciating what one already has. The story encourages readers to cultivate a sense of gratitude and to prioritize the well-being of others, rather than pursuing wealth and material possessions at any cost.
