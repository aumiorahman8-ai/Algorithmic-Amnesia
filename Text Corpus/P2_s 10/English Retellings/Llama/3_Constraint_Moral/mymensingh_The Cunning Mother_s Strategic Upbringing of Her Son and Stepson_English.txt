In the Bengali folktale, a cunning mother raises her son and stepson with a strategic approach to teach them valuable life lessons. She gives her son a pot of milk and tells him to sell it in the market, but warns him that if he fails, he will be punished. Meanwhile, she gives her stepson a pot of milk with a hole in it, making it impossible to sell. When the stepson returns empty-handed, she scolds him and praises her son for his success.

However, the next day, she switches their tasks. She gives her stepson a whole pot of milk and her son a pot with a hole. This time, the stepson succeeds, and the son fails. The mother then reveals her plan, teaching both sons that success and failure are not solely dependent on individual abilities, but also on the opportunities and challenges presented.

The moral of the story emphasizes the importance of empathy, fairness, and equal opportunities. The cunning mother's strategy highlights that every individual deserves a chance to succeed, and that circumstances can greatly impact one's achievements. The tale promotes a sense of justice, encouraging parents and caregivers to provide equal support and opportunities to all children, regardless of their background or relationship.
