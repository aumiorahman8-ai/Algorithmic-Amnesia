In the Bengali folktale "The Stepmother's Misguided Affection," a stepmother's excessive love for her own daughter leads her to mistreat her stepdaughter. The stepmother's actions ultimately result in her own daughter's downfall. The story teaches valuable lessons about the importance of fairness, equality, and the consequences of misguided affection.

The tale highlights the dangers of favoritism and the need for parents to treat all their children with equal love and care. The stepmother's biased behavior creates a toxic environment, leading to the stepdaughter's suffering and the daughter's eventual demise. The story also emphasizes the importance of selflessness and the need to prioritize the well-being of all children, regardless of their biological relationships.

Ultimately, the folktale warns against the destructive nature of excessive attachment and the importance of finding a balance in parental love. It encourages parents to cultivate a sense of fairness, empathy, and understanding, ensuring that all children feel valued and loved. By doing so, parents can create a harmonious and nurturing environment, allowing their children to thrive and grow into capable and compassionate individuals. The story's moral lessons remain relevant today, serving as a reminder of the importance of responsible and selfless parenting.
