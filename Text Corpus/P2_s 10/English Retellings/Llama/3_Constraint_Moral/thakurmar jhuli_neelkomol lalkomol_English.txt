"Neelkomol Lalkomol" is a traditional Bengali folktale that teaches valuable lessons about honesty, kindness, and the consequences of one's actions. The story revolves around two friends, Neelkomol and Lalkomol, who are as different as night and day. Neelkomol is a hardworking and honest farmer, while Lalkomol is lazy and deceitful.

One day, a king announces that he will reward anyone who can bring him a basket of golden apples. Lalkomol tricks Neelkomol into giving him his share of the apples, but ultimately, his dishonesty leads to his downfall. The king, impressed by Neelkomol's honesty and kindness, rewards him with wealth and prosperity.

The tale conveys several important morals, including the importance of honesty, the value of hard work, and the consequences of deceit. It also highlights the rewards of kindness and generosity. The story teaches children that honesty is always the best policy and that hard work and kindness will ultimately lead to success and happiness. The contrast between Neelkomol and Lalkomol serves as a reminder that our actions have consequences, and that choosing the right path is essential for a fulfilling life.
