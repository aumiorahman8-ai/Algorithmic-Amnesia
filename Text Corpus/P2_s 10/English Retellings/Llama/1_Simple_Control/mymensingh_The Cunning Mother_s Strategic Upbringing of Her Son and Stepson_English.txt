The Bengali folktale 'The Cunning Mother's Strategic Upbringing of Her Son and Stepson' is a story about a clever and resourceful mother who devises a plan to ensure the well-being and prosperity of both her biological son and her stepson.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a wise and cunning mother who had a son from her previous marriage. She later married a widower with a son of his own. The mother, determined to provide the best possible life for both her biological son and her stepson, decided to raise them in a unique and strategic manner.

She divided her property and wealth into two equal parts, one for each boy. However, she did not give them the property directly. Instead, she devised a plan to teach them valuable life skills and lessons that would help them succeed in the future.

The mother told her sons that they would have to work hard to earn their share of the property. She sent her biological son to study and become a scholar, while her stepson was trained in the art of warfare and combat. The mother's reasoning was that her biological son, being book-smart, would be able to navigate the complexities of the world through his knowledge, while her stepson, being skilled in combat, would be able to protect himself and their family from any physical harm.

As the years passed, both sons grew up to be skilled and capable individuals. The biological son became a renowned scholar, and the stepson became a brave and powerful warrior. The mother's plan had worked, and both sons were well-equipped to face the challenges of the world.

One day, the king of the land announced that he was looking for a wise and knowledgeable advisor to help him govern the kingdom. The mother's biological son, with his vast knowledge and scholarly background, was the perfect candidate for the job. He was appointed as the king's advisor and soon became a trusted and influential figure in the kingdom.

Meanwhile, the stepson, with his combat skills, joined the king's army and quickly rose through the ranks. He became a respected and feared warrior, known for his bravery and strength.

The mother's strategic upbringing had paid off, and both sons had achieved great success and prosperity. The biological son, as the king's advisor, was able to provide for the family and ensure their well-being, while the stepson, as a warrior, was able to protect them from any harm.

The story of 'The Cunning Mother's Strategic Upbringing of Her Son and Stepson' teaches us the importance of planning, hard work, and strategic thinking in achieving success and prosperity. It also highlights the value of a mother's love and devotion in shaping the lives of her children and ensuring their well-being.
