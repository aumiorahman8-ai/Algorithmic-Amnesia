A village headman passed away, and his son, who was greedy for influence and prestige, tried to take his place. However, the son lacked the necessary knowledge and intelligence for the role. He had a nephew who acted as his sycophant and advisor. The nephew warned the new headman that his tendency to tell unrealistic stories would make people lose respect for him, so they devised a system where the nephew would make a "hmm" sound to signal the headman to stop exaggerating.

During a village council meeting, the headman started telling tall tales, claiming that a massive snake, 100 cubits long, had appeared in his bamboo grove. When someone questioned the impossibility of this, the nephew made the "hmm" sound, prompting the headman to reduce the snake's length to 90 cubits, then 80, and eventually 70 cubits, each time being signaled by the nephew to be more realistic. However, the headman became angry and refused to go lower than 60 cubits.

The absurdity of the situation caused everyone present to burst into laughter, and they reprimanded the headman for his foolishness. As a result, he was never seen at a village council or judgment again, his lack of wisdom and intelligence having been exposed.
