In a certain land, a king's daughter was captured by a giant python and taken to the sea, where she was placed on the python's head. The king, desperate for her rescue, announced that whoever could save her would be rewarded with half the kingdom and her hand in marriage. Four brothers, each with a unique skill - a carpenter, a boatman, a hunter, and a robber - heard the news and decided to take on the challenge. They built a boat, rowed it to the sea, and worked together to rescue the princess, with the hunter killing the python and the robber retrieving the princess from its head. Since the robber was the first to touch and carry the princess, the king declared him the rightful claimant and awarded him the princess in marriage, along with half of his wealth and property. The other three brothers accepted this decision, recognizing the robber as the primary rescuer and themselves as his helpers. The robber brother ultimately married the princess, claiming his reward for rescuing her from the python.
