A father had five sons and one daughter. The sons decided to cut a ghila creeper in one stroke to gain renown. They conspired against their father, blunting his machete, and when they succeeded in cutting the creeper, their father failed. As per their oath, the father had to live in a hut by the tiger's path. The sons built the hut and left their father there. The father scared away the tigers for two nights by shouting and describing himself, but on the third night, he fell asleep and was killed by the tigers.

The daughter discovered her father's death and informed her brothers, who decided to avenge their father's murder. They prepared their bows and arrows but failed to hit a rooster and a Mala pig in the villages they passed through. They eventually reached a Debi's house, where they were fed their father's flesh without knowing it. Debi destroyed the sharpness of their arrows, and when they tried to fight the tigers she called, they failed and became the tigers' prey.

The daughter was left alone and, after eating an amloki that fell from the sky, became pregnant and gave birth to a son named Suanlo Suanlo. As Suanlo grew up, his mother taught him to avenge his uncles' murder. Suanlo practiced and prepared himself, and when the time came, he set out to avenge his uncles. He passed through the same villages as his uncles and finally reached Debi's house. This time, Debi's plan to blunt his arrows failed because they were made of stone. Suanlo killed all the tigers, including the pregnant chief tigress, and took revenge for his uncles.

When Suanlo returned home, he found his mother engaged in an improper act and was unable to meet her. He left the proof of his revenge, the tigress's head, and a rooster chick, and called upon his father in the sky to lower a ladder for him. Suanlo climbed the ladder and disappeared into the sky. His mother, filled with grief and regret, eventually died after being injured by the tigress's tooth while handling the head.
