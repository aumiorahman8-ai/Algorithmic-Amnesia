In a far-off country, there lived a beautiful prince who was admired by everyone in the kingdom. One day, the prince decided to travel to foreign lands, which caused his parents, the king and queen, great distress. Despite their concerns, the prince set out on his journey, dressed in new clothes and carrying a new sword.

He traveled through many countries, mountains, and rivers, until he arrived in the middle of a dense forest. As he walked deeper into the forest, he came across a magnificent royal palace that seemed to stretch on forever. The palace was eerily silent, with no signs of life or music. The prince cautiously entered the palace and found it to be spotlessly clean, but completely empty.

As he explored the palace, he discovered a vast courtyard filled with stone statues of elephants, horses, and soldiers. He also found chambers filled with shields, swords, and other treasures, but all the people were stone statues. The prince continued to explore, eventually coming across a beautiful princess sleeping in a golden lotus flower.

The prince was captivated by the princess's beauty and watched her for many years, unable to look away. One day, he noticed a golden stick and a silver stick near the princess's head. As he picked up the sticks, the golden stick accidentally tapped the princess's head, awakening her from her deep sleep.

The princess's awakening had a profound effect on the entire palace, as all the stone statues came to life, and the kingdom was restored to its former glory. The king, minister, and courtiers were overjoyed to be awakened from their thousand-year sleep and thanked the prince for saving them.

As a reward, the king offered the prince the princess's hand in marriage and the entire kingdom. The prince and princess were married in a grand ceremony, and they ruled the kingdom together. Eventually, the prince returned to his own kingdom, bringing his new wife with him. His parents, the king and queen, were overjoyed to see their son return, and the prince used the golden stick to restore his father's sight and cure his mother's illness. The prince, princess, king, and queen then ruled their kingdom together, filled with happiness and joy.
