A young boy caught a Shol fish during the monsoon using a hook. He took it home and gave it to his mother, who began to clean it by scraping off its scales with ash and cutting its gills. However, before she could finish, she left it unattended to use the bathroom, and a kite swooped down, snatched the fish, and flew away. The kite accidentally dropped the fish into a pond, where it began swimming. As it swam, it saw some young boys trying to catch fish with their hooks and warned them about the dangers of being caught, recounting its own ordeal of being hooked, cleaned, and carried away by the kite.
