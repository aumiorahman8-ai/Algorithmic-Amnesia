A husband and wife had been quarreling for two days and weren't speaking to each other. The wife wanted her husband to go fishing, as many villagers were doing, but couldn't ask him directly due to their quarrel. Instead, she made a comment about people going to the wetlands with polo traps and asked who would go for her. The husband responded by saying he was hungry and couldn't go. The wife then told him to serve himself from the available food, but the husband complained that the rice was a mess. The wife, frustrated with his excuses, eventually told him to swallow his pride and eat.
