A woman had two sons, one of whom was her own child and the other belonged to her co-wife. Despite this, she showed more affection to her co-wife's son, often carrying him on her lap and back, while neglecting her own son and leaving him to play in the dust and sand. When a neighboring woman questioned her about this unusual behavior, the mother explained that her actions were intentional. She believed that by constantly carrying her co-wife's son, she was making him weak and helpless, whereas by leaving her own son to fend for himself in the dirt, she was teaching him to be strong and robust.
