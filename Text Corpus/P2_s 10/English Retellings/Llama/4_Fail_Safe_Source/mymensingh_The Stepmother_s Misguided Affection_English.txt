A man married and had a son with his first wife, but she passed away. To care for his son, he remarried, instructing his new wife to raise his son with affection and not cause him any pain. The second wife agreed and initially showed great care to her stepson, giving him preferential treatment over her own three sons. She sent the stepson to school while her own sons worked in the fields, resulting in the stepson becoming a student and her sons becoming farmers.

As time passed, the stepson stopped studying, got married, and had children. He then began selling his share of the family land, which the second wife's sons bought. Eventually, the stepson was left with nothing. The man realized that his second wife's idea of "affection" had ultimately led to his son's downfall, and he criticized her for not raising him in a way that would have prepared him for a better life.
