**The Princess, the Python, and the Four Brothers – A Short Retelling**

In a kingdom by the river lived a kind princess who loved the forest. One day a massive python seized her while she was gathering flowers, dragging her toward its lair. The king sent his bravest warriors, but none could free her; the python’s coils were too strong.

Four brothers—each a humble farmer—heard the news while working their fields. Though they had no weapons, they possessed cleverness, patience, and unity. The eldest suggested they lure the python with a fragrant offering; the second proposed they distract it with music; the third thought they should untie the knot of its coils; the youngest offered to pray for divine help.

Together they set a basket of ripe mangoes at the python’s mouth. While the beast feasted, the second brother played a flute, soothing the creature. The third brother, using a sharp sickle, carefully cut the tightest coil, and the youngest whispered a prayer for the princess’s safety. The python, now content and unthreatened, released the princess and slithered away.

The princess thanked the brothers, and the king rewarded them with land and honor. The kingdom celebrated the triumph of wit, cooperation, and humility over brute force.

**Morals / Lessons**

1. **Unity is Strength** – The brothers’ combined talents succeeded where a single hero could not.  
2. **Intelligence Over Violence** – Clever, peaceful strategies can defeat even the fiercest foes.  
3. **Humility and Service** – Ordinary people, when modest and helpful, can achieve greatness.  
4. **Compassion Wins** – Treating the python with kindness broke its aggression, showing empathy can resolve conflict.  

Thus, the tale reminds us that collaboration, wisdom, and a gentle heart are the true weapons against danger.
