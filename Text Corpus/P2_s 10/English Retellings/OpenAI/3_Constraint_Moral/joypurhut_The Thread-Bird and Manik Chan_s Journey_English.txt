**The Thread‑Bird and Manik Chan’s Journey**  

Manik Chan, a modest village boy, set out to seek his fortune. Before leaving, an old weaver warned him to treat every creature with respect, for even the smallest being may become a great helper. On the road Manik rescued a tiny, trembling bird tangled in a thorny bush. Grateful, the bird—known as the Thread‑Bird for its golden, silk‑like plumage—offered to guide him.

The Thread‑Bird led Manik through dense forests, across swift rivers, and past hungry wolves, always finding a safe path. When they reached a towering mountain, the bird taught Manik to weave a rope from its own shimmering feathers, showing that resourcefulness can turn a humble gift into a lifeline. Together they descended, and the bird warned Manik not to accept riches without humility.  

At a bustling market, a merchant offered Manik a chest of gold for the bird’s feathers. Remembering the weaver’s counsel, Manik refused, saying that true wealth lay in kindness and the friendships earned on his journey. The merchant, moved by his honesty, gave Manik a modest purse and a promise of future trade.

Manik returned home, richer in spirit than in coin. He shared the Thread‑Bird’s lesson: **respect all life, cherish modest gifts, and let generosity guide ambition.** The tale endures as a reminder that compassion and humility are the true threads that bind a successful life.
