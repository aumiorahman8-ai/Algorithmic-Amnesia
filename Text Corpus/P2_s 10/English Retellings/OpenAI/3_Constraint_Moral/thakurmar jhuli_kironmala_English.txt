**Kironmala – A Tale of Kindness, Duty, and Wisdom**

In a small Bengali village lived Kironmala, a gentle, widowed potter’s wife renowned for her compassion. Every dawn she collected wild herbs, boiled rice, and offered a modest meal to the wandering hermit who passed by, never asking for anything in return. One monsoon night, a fierce storm flooded the river, sweeping away the village’s granaries. The villagers panicked, but Kironmala calmly gathered the few jars of dried fish she kept for emergencies and shared them equally, even with strangers who had taken shelter in her modest hut.

Impressed, the hermit revealed himself as a sage and gifted her a tiny, shining seed, saying, “Plant it with a pure heart, and it will bring abundance for all.” Kironmala sowed the seed beside the communal well, tending it daily with humility. Soon a golden vine sprouted, bearing fruit that fed the entire hamlet through the next harsh winter.

When the king’s envoys demanded the miracle for the palace, Kironmala refused, explaining that the vine’s magic belonged to those who needed it most. The envoys left humbled, and the sage smiled, for Kironmala’s self‑less deeds had already fulfilled the true purpose of the gift.

**Lessons:**  
1. **Generosity without expectation** creates lasting prosperity.  
2. **Humility and service** turn ordinary people into custodians of miracles.  
3. **Wisdom lives in simple hearts**; true power is shared, not hoarded.  
4. **Respect for nature and community** yields blessings that sustain all.
