A woman lived with two boys. One of the children was the son of her co‑wife, while the other was her own. She adored the co‑wife’s son, constantly holding him on her lap and carrying him wherever she went. In contrast, she ignored her own son, leaving him in the dust and sand and never picking him up. 

One day a neighboring woman noticed this and said to the mother, “Sister, I see you never show affection to your own son but you dote on your co‑wife’s son. Why do you act that way? I have never seen you do this before.” 

The mother answered, “Sister, you wouldn’t understand. By keeping my co‑wife’s son constantly in my arms I am making him weak and helpless. By leaving my own son out in the dust and dirt, I am raising him to be strong and robust.”
