A husband and wife had quarreled and hadn't spoken a word to each other for two days. Outside, many villagers were passing by the house with polo traps, heading to the wetlands to catch fish. Seeing this, the wife thought to herself that she should get her husband to go fishing too. But because of their quarrel, she couldn't speak to him directly.

So, the husband and wife held this conversation:

Wife: "All the folks are off with their polo traps to the fen; But who is there to go for me? Which of the men?"

Husband: "I haven't had a bite nor a touch of food, you see; So how am I supposed to go? You tell me."

Wife: "The rice and curry are in the shika pot, right there; Why don't you serve yourself and have your share?"

Husband: "Some grains are long, some are short—it's a mess complete; How, tell me, am I supposed to eat?"

Wife: "Just mould a mouthful atop your insults, and swallow it whole!"
