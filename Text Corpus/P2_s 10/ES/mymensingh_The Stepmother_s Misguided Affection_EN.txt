A man got married. His wife died, leaving behind a son. Thinking of his son, the man did not want to marry again. But a woman was needed to run the household, so he remarried. On their wedding night, however, he told his second wife, "You cannot cause my son any pain. You must raise him with your affection." The wife replied, "Husband, I will follow your words to the letter."

The man's second wife gave birth to three more sons. The wife began to raise her stepson with great care, carrying him in her lap and on her back, while her own children were left on the floor.

She sent her stepson to school, but sent her own sons to plow the fields. In time, the stepson became a student, while her own sons became farmers. After a while, it was seen that the stepson gave up his studies and became obsessed with getting married. After convincing her husband, she arranged for the stepson to be married. He also had children. A few years later, he started selling his share of the family land. Her sons bought it. In this way, a time came when her stepson was left destitute. Then the man said to his wife, "This is not the kind of affection I told you to raise him with."
