In a certain land, there was a king. The king had a daughter. One day, a giant python captured the king's daughter and took her to the sea. In the middle of the sea, the python placed the princess on its head. The king became restless. He had a proclamation beaten by drum throughout his kingdom for his daughter's rescue. He announced that whoever could rescue his daughter from the sea would be given half the kingdom and her hand in marriage. Hearing this news, four brothers from one family prepared to rescue the princess. The first brother was a carpenter, the second a boatman who plied the boat, the third a hunter, and the fourth a robber. These four brothers responded to the king's proclamation and went to inform the king of their intentions. The king summoned them. As per the king's conditions, they set out to sea to rescue the princess. The carpenter built a boat, the boatman rowed it, the robber took the king's daughter from the serpent's head, and the hunter killed the serpent. The four brothers, each using his own skill, brought the king's daughter back from the python's head. Now, each of them, by virtue of his own merit, was a claimant to the princess and half the kingdom. So, who was to get the princess? Since it was the robber who had first touched the princess and carried her back, the king gave her to him in marriage, along with half of his wealth and property. In the rescue of the princess, the robber was declared the rightful claimant. The others were considered his helpers. And so, the matter was settled. The other three brothers understood and accepted this. The robber brother married the princess, thus claiming his reward.
