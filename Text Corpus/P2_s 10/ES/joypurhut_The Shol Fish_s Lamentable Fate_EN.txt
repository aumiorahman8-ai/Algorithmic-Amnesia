The pitiful plight of a Shol fish. During the monsoon, water was overflowing everywhere. It was a time when young boys would go fishing with their hooks. Wherever they spotted the slightest movement of fish, they would cast their lines. A young boy was bobbing his baited hook up and down in the water when a Shol fish got caught. He brought it home and gave it to his mother to prepare. The mother used ash to scrape off the fish's scales. Then, she cut and cleaned its two gills. At that moment, she had to go to the bathroom. At that time, a kite was flying overhead. It spotted the fish and was overcome with the desire to have it. As it swooped down and flew away with the fish, it fell from its beak into a pond. The Shol fish was swimming around the edge of the pond. On the bank, young boys were bobbing their hooks in the water, trying to catch fish. Seeing this, the Shol fish addressed them and said:

I broke through twelve bites and thirteen pokes,
In the splashing fish trap, my head was left bare.
Rolled in the dust, with both my gills cut,
That rascal kite carried me across three worlds.
He who does not know the wound of the bobbing bait,
Go and bob your hook near him.
