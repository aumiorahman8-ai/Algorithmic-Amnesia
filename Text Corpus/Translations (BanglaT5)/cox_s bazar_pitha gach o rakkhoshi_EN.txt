The only boy who lost his father when he was a child was living with his mother. From childhood, he loved cakes. Every day his mother had to make cakes for him, or the boy would be very sad. But it became impossible for his depressed mother. The last time she made cakes, she planted a cake on one side of the house. A few days later, a plant grew up.
In the middle of the journey, the mongoose slipped into the jungle beside the road to respond to the natural call. The boy slipped out of the sack and shut it up. The mongoose found the sack and put it on his shoulder. One day, the mongoose found the sack and started to slide it off. The mongoose wanted to slide it off. The mongoose used to say, 'It's dirty'.
