A headman of a certain village passed away. His only son grew greedy for his father's influence and prestige, so he tried to become the headman himself. But he lacked the necessary knowledge and intelligence. He kept a distant nephew by his side as a sycophant. Whenever anything happened in the village, he would jump at the opportunity to assert his authority. He would also go around boasting and telling all sorts of unrealistic stories. One day, the nephew advised him that if he kept telling such unrealistic stories, people would realize his lack of intelligence and no one would respect him. Consequently, they decided that whenever he started to exaggerate, the nephew would make a "hmm" sound to signal him to check himself. One day, while presiding over a village council meeting, this new headman was telling tall tales. At one point, he exclaimed, "Yesterday, a massive snake appeared in my bamboo grove! It was 100 cubits long!"
Someone from the side said, "That's impossible." The nephew then made a "hmm" sound. Realizing his mistake, the new headman declared, "Well, it couldn't have been less than 90 cubits." The nephew again made the "hmm" sound. The headman said, "Even if it wasn't 90 cubits, it was definitely 80." Someone from the side said, "That's also impossible." The nephew signaled again. This time the new headman exclaimed, "Well then, it must have been 70 cubits—absolutely no less than that!" The nephew signaled once more. At this, the headman grew angry and said, "Nephew, no matter how much you haggle, I'm not going any lower than 60 cubits!" Realizing the extent of his wisdom, everyone present burst into loud laughter. Everyone reprimanded him, and as a result, the headman left the meeting. From that day on, he was never seen at a village council or judgment again.
