A husband and wife had a quarrel and have not been speaking to each other for two days. Many people are passing by their house with fishing traps, going to the wetlands to catch fish. Seeing this, the wife thinks to herself about getting her husband to go catch fish. But because of their quarrel, she is unable to speak to him. The conversation between the husband and wife in this situation:

Wife: Everyone is going with their fishing traps, who is there to go for me?
Husband: I haven't eaten a thing, how am I supposed to go?
Wife: The rice and curry are ready, why don't you eat then?
Husband: Some pieces are long, some are short. How am I supposed to eat this?
Wife: Then pile one insult on top of another and eat.
