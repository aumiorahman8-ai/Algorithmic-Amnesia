A man killed his enemy. After the murder, he dug a hole in a spot not far from his house and buried the body. Several years passed. With no trace of the murderer found, everyone eventually forgot about the incident. One day, during the height of the monsoon, the man was sitting on his veranda, cutting jute. He was gazing straight from his doorway towards that very spot. After so long, the body had decomposed, and bubbles were rising from the ground there. Seeing this, he was smiling to himself. His wife noticed this repeatedly. When she asked why he was smiling to himself, he said it was for no reason. But his wife was not satisfied. So, when she relentlessly pressed him, the man finally said, "They say that sin can never stay hidden. But I see that saying is false. See those bubbles rising from the ground over there? That's where I killed so-and-so (name) and buried him. It's been several years, has anyone figured out that I am the killer? So, when I thought about that, I couldn't help but laugh." Some time later, the man had a terrible argument with his wife. At one point, in the heat of her anger, the wife shouted, "You are a terrible man, you murdered so-and-so (name)!" Instantly, the news spread throughout the entire village. The relatives of the murdered person reported the matter to the police and filed a case. The police came and arrested him. In the end, he was punished.
