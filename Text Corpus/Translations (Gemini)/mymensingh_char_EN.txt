A woman had two sons. One was her co-wife’s, and the other was her own. Of the two children, she greatly adored her co-wife's son. She would always carry him on her lap and back. Her own son, however, she would leave in the dust and sand. She never picked him up or carried him. Seeing this, a neighboring woman one day said to the mother, “Sister, I’ve noticed something. You don’t show affection to your own son, but you dote on your co-wife’s son. What’s this about? I’ve only ever seen you do such a thing.” The mother of the two boys replied, “Ah, sister, you wouldn’t understand. By keeping my co-wife’s son constantly in my arms, I am making him weak and helpless. But by leaving my son in the dust and dirt, I am raising him to be strong and robust.”
