There was once a man who murdered his enemy. After the killing, he dug a pit in a spot not far from his house and buried the body. Several years passed. Since no clue was ever found regarding the killer, everyone eventually forgot the incident.

One day, in the height of the monsoon, the man was sitting on his veranda, stripping jute fibers. He was staring straight out his door toward that very spot. After such a long time, the body had decomposed, and gurgling bubbles were rising from the earth. Seeing this, he began to smile to himself.

His wife noticed this repeatedly. When she asked why he was laughing to himself, he said it was for no reason. But his wife was not satisfied. So, when she pressed him relentlessly, the man finally spoke:

"They say that 'Sin never stays hidden.' But I see now that the saying is false. Look at those bubbles gurgling up from the ground over there? That is where I killed [Name] and buried him. See, it has been several years; has anyone been able to catch me as the killer? When I thought of that, I couldn't help but laugh."

Some time later, the man had a terrible quarrel with his wife. At one point, in a fit of rage, the wife screamed out loud, "You are a wicked man! You murdered [Name]!"

Instantly, the word spread like wildfire throughout the entire village. The relatives of the murdered man reported the matter to the station and filed a case. The police came and arrested him. In the end, he received his punishment.
